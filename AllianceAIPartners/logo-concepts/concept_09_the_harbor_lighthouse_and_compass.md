# Concept 9: The Harbor Lighthouse & Compass — "The Calm Expert in the Room"

**Brand Identity & Figma Logo System Specification · Alliance AI Partners (Brand DNA v2.1)**  
**Methodology Reference:** [Figma's 12 Types of Logos Framework](https://www.figma.com/resource-library/types-of-logos/)

## 1. Strategic Foundation & Logo Idea

* **Core Metaphor:** Grounded in Personality §5 (*"If Alliance AI Partners were a person, they'd be the calm expert in the room. They know AI well, explain it in plain words, and take responsibility for the results. In brand-strategy terms, our main archetype is the Sage: the wise guide who tells you the truth. Our second archetype is the Ruler: the steady leader who brings order"*), Core Value #3 (*"If AI isn't the right answer, we say so, even when it means less work for us"*), and the historic masonry **Farola / Lighthouses of the Philippine Archipelago** (such as Cape Bojeador and Cape Engaño) that have guided international vessels safely through treacherous straits for centuries. Visually, a tapered stone lighthouse tower rises to form the outer legs and lantern crown of a monumental capital **`A`** in Maritime Night Navy (`#0F1E2E`), while two crisp horizontal-diverging signal beams cut across the waist as the crossbar of the **`A`**, anchored by a Nautical Brass (`#6E4F1A`) and Beacon Crimson (`#9E2A2B`) cardinal compass point. It tells founders lost in AI hype: *We are your steady navigation beacon. We tell you where the rocks are and guide you safely into harbor.*
* **Master Mark Recommendation (Static vs. Dynamic):** **Hybrid Static-Tower + Dynamic-Beacon System.** The primary corporate mark uses a **Static Lighthouse-Tower `A`** where the tapered masonry tower and horizontal signal-beam crossbar remain fixed across corporate stationery, contracts, and headers. In interactive UI states, diagnostic tools (*The AI check-up*), and service pillar headers (Type 12), the beacon lens and compass beam rotate or shift across three cardinal azimuths (*West/North-West: Scale AI*, *North: Solve problems with AI*, *East/North-East: Grow*).
* **Primary Sketch / Geometry Concept:**
  * **Base Construction Grid:** Engineered on a `24×24px` square grid (`240×240px` vector master) with a tapered architectural batter angle (`76°` outer walls narrowing from `14px` wide at base `y = 22` to `6px` wide at the lantern gallery `y = 4`).
  * **Lighthouse `A` Tower:** Two solid tapered masonry flanks in Maritime Night Navy (`#0F1E2E`) rise from a `2px` seawall plinth (`y = 20..22`) to a pitched lantern dome (`y = 2..5`).
  * **Beacon Crossbar & Compass Core:** At `y = 10..14`, a clean horizontal negative-space beam slices across the tower while a sharp diamond-shaped **Compass Rose / Fresnel Core** in Beacon Signal Crimson (`#9E2A2B`) sits centered inside the lantern chamber, forming the unmistakable apex-and-crossbar geometry of a capital **`A`**.

## 3. Design System & UI Integration Overview

* **Typography System:**
  * **Display / Headings:** **Fraunces** or **Instrument Serif** (`600 SemiBold`, tracking `-0.02em`, line-height `1.14`) paired with **Plus Jakarta Sans** (`600 SemiBold`, `700 Bold`). Gives headlines the calm, authoritative voice of a seasoned captain's log or executive briefing (*"Before we suggest anything, walk us through what happens when a new customer request comes in."*).
  * **Body & UI Controls:** **Inter** (Weights: `400 Regular`, `500 Medium`, `600 SemiBold`; Base Size: `16px`; Line-Height: `1.6`). Plain-English legibility with zero jargon.
  * **Monospace / Pricing & Data:** **JetBrains Mono** (`500 Medium`). Used for compass bearings, *AI check-up* ROI estimates, published starting prices, and verified international salary benchmarks.
* **Layout Grid & Spacing Tokens:**
  * **Desktop Grid:** `12-column` admiralty chart grid (`1280px` max width, `24px` gutters) framed by subtle degree/tick marks along the outer container border and anchored by a sticky left-hand "Navigation Compass Index" on long-form guides.
  * **Spacing Scale:** `8px` spatial rhythm (`8px`, `16px`, `24px`, `32px`, `48px`, `64px`, `96px`).
  * **Border & Radius Tokens:** `4px` card radius, `2px` button radius, and `1px solid #C8D4E0` borders with small `45°` compass-diamond corner markers on featured advisory callouts.
* **Key UI Component Guidelines:**
  * **Navigation & Logo Placement:** `72px` header on `#F8F9FA` featuring the **Lighthouse `A` Combination Mark** on the left, plain-English navigation items in the center, and the Beacon Signal Crimson (`#9E2A2B`) primary CTA button (*"See where AI can help"*) on the right—leading directly to the low-risk **AI check-up** first project.
  * **Honest Pricing Cards & Comparison Tables:** Styled as an "Admiralty Soundings & True Cost Chart" on `#EBF0F5`. Displays side-by-side comparisons of how other AI firms charge—hourly rates (`US$100–$149/hr` in US/AU via [Clutch Web Developers](https://clutch.co/web-developers/pricing) and [Clutch Web Designers](https://clutch.co/web-designers/pricing)), per-seat licenses (`US$30/user/mo` via [Microsoft 365 Copilot](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/copilot/enterprise)), per-resolution fees (`US$0.99/outcome` via [Intercom](https://www.intercom.com/pricing) and `US$150/mo + $2.50/call` via [Smith.ai](https://smith.ai/pricing/ai-receptionist)), and retainers (`US$3,199/mo` avg via [Clutch SEO](https://clutch.co/seo-firms/pricing))—against Alliance's honest fixed quotes and embedded developers (benchmarked transparently against US `$196,500/yr` via [BLS](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/computer-and-information-technology/software-developers.htm) & [BLS ECEC](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/ecec.t04.htm), UK `£69,400/yr` via [ONS](https://www.ons.gov.uk/employmentandlabourmarket/peopleinwork/earningsandworkinghours/bulletins/annualsurveyofhoursandearnings/2026) & [UK Gov](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/rates-and-thresholds-for-employers-2026-to-2027), and Australia `A$147,800/yr` via [Jobs and Skills Australia](https://www.jobsandskills.gov.au/data/occupation-and-industry-profiles/occupations-anzsco/2613-software-and-applications-programmers) & [ATO](https://www.ato.gov.au/tax-rates-and-codes/key-superannuation-rates-and-thresholds/super-guarantee), supported by [IBPAP](https://admin.ibpap.org/storage/hub-resources/7Hv9U2uoLJVx2kxyRMObNm6W6L0MR57l9dZfrez7.pdf), [EF EPI 2025](https://www.ef.com/wwen/epi/regions/asia/philippines/), and [RA 10173](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/2/50253)). Includes an explicit "Honest Counsel Guarantee" callout: *"If an off-the-shelf tool or a simpler fix costs you less—or if AI isn't the right answer—we say so."*
  * **Service Pillar Cards (Scale, Solve, Grow):** Organized as a **Three-Point Compass Rose Selector** (*Bearing 01: Scale AI*, *Bearing 02: Solve problems with AI*, *Bearing 03: Grow*) above the 11 industry solution packages (with dedicated privacy-review badges on Healthcare, Legal, Finance, and Insurance).

## 5. Figma Best-Practices Verification Scorecard

* **App-Icon Scalability (`16×16px` & `60×60px`):** **PASS.** Consists of a bold tapered `A` silhouette, a `2px` horizontal negative-space beam crossbar, and a straight-edged `4×4px` central diamond (`#9E2A2B`). Note: Because the diamond uses straight `45°` edges rather than concave curves, it never resembles a prohibited "AI sparkle" and snaps cleanly to pixel grids at `16×16px`.
* **Monochrome (`1-Bit Black/White`) Test:** **PASS.** The horizontal negative-space beam channel separates the upper lantern dome from the lower tower legs, and a `1.5px` negative-space collar frames the central diamond—ensuring 100% clarity in 1-bit `#000000` on `#FFFFFF`.
* **Readability & Contrast Audit:** **PASS.** All three core brand colors pass **WCAG AAA (`> 7.0:1`)** on the light surface (`#F8F9FA`): Maritime Night Navy (`#0F1E2E`) at `16.00:1`, Beacon Signal Crimson (`#9E2A2B`) at `7.06:1`, and Nautical Sextant Brass (`#6E4F1A`) at `7.12:1`.
