# Concept 13: The Prism Foundation — "Turning Complex Problems Into Three Clear Actions"

**Brand Identity & Figma Logo System Specification · Alliance AI Partners (Brand DNA v2.1)**  
**Methodology Reference:** [Figma's 12 Types of Logos Framework](https://www.figma.com/resource-library/types-of-logos/)

## 1. Strategic Foundation & Logo Idea

* **Core Metaphor:** Grounded in Personality §5 (*"We are: Clear, never hard to follow. We use everyday words and explain any technical term we need"*), Mission §3 (*"1. Understand. We learn how their business works and where it gets stuck. 2. Plan. We pick the right fix"*), and Note 1 (*"On the website and in sales calls, lead with the three goals—scale AI, solve problems, and grow—and let people open the full list from there"*). In optical physics, an equilateral triangular **Prism** takes a single complex, unstructured beam of light on the left and refracts it cleanly into distinct, ordered bands on the right. Visually, a monolithic equilateral triangular **Prism `A`** in Monolith Basalt (`#16181D`) receives a single horizontal input bar on its left flank (representing the partner's messy operational bottleneck) and splits it across the interior crossbar of the **`A`** into **three crisp, parallel geometric output bands** representing **1. Scale AI**, **2. Solve problems with AI**, and **3. Grow** (rendered in solid, flat, WCAG-verified architectural tones—never a blurry rainbow or purple gradient).
* **Master Mark Recommendation (Static vs. Dynamic):** **Hybrid Static-Prism + Dynamic-Refraction System.** The primary corporate mark uses a **Static Equilateral Prism `A`** where the single left input bar refracts into three parallel right-hand crossbar bands inside the silhouette of the `A`. Across digital headers, interactive service selectors, and diagnostic tools (Type 12), each of the three refracted output bands extends outward to highlight the active service goal (*Scale AI*, *Solve problems with AI*, or *Grow*).
* **Primary Sketch / Geometry Concept:**
  * **Base Construction Grid:** Engineered on a `24×24px` grid (`240×240px` vector master) anchored by a `60°` equilateral triangular prism (`x = 3..21, y = 3..21`) with a `3px` heavy monolithic frame in `#16181D`.
  * **Single Input -> Three-Goal Output Crossbar:**
    * **Left Flank (Input):** A single solid `3px` horizontal bar enters the left leg of the `A` at `y = 13`.
    * **Interior & Right Flank (Refracted 3-Goal Crossbar):** Inside the triangular vault of the `A`, the single bar fans cleanly at `+15°`, `0°`, and `-15°` (or three parallel stepped horizontal bars separated by `1.5px` negative-space slits) exiting through the right leg in `#1E468A` (*Scale AI*), `#16181D` (*Solve problems with AI*), and `#19614B` (*Grow*).

## 3. Design System & UI Integration Overview

* **Typography System:**
  * **Display / Headings:** **Plus Jakarta Sans** or **Outfit** (`600 SemiBold`, `700 Bold`; Tracking: `-0.025em`; Line-Height: `1.14`). Clean triangular apexes and crisp geometric strokes mirror the `60°` equilateral prism.
  * **Body & UI Controls:** **Inter** (Weights: `400 Regular`, `500 Medium`, `600 SemiBold`; Base Size: `16px`; Line-Height: `1.6`). Embodied clarity—turning dense technical concepts into plain everyday words.
  * **Monospace / Pricing & Data:** **JetBrains Mono** (`500 Medium`). Used for diagnostic metrics, published starting prices, and verified international salary comparisons.
* **Layout Grid & Spacing Tokens:**
  * **Desktop Grid:** `12-column` "1-Into-3" diagnostic grid (`1280px` max width, `24px` gutters): major sections feature a `3-column` left-hand "Problem / Input Summary" column that fans out rightward into **three `3-column` Solution Cards** (*Scale AI*, *Solve problems with AI*, *Grow*).
  * **Spacing Scale:** `8px` spatial unit (`8px`, `16px`, `24px`, `32px`, `48px`, `64px`, `96px`).
  * **Border & Radius Tokens:** `4px` card radius, `2px` button radius, with signature 3-stripe left-edge indicator bars (`#1E468A`, `#16181D`, `#19614B`) on summary containers.
* **Key UI Component Guidelines:**
  * **Navigation & Logo Placement:** `72px` header on `#F9FAFC` with the **Prism `A` Combination Mark** on the left, clean navigation links in the center, and the Refracted Indigo-Cobalt (`#1E468A`) primary CTA button (*"See where AI can help"*) on the right.
  * **Honest Pricing Cards & Comparison Tables:** Designed as an "Optical Clarity Price Breakdown" on `#EEF0F5`. Splits every pricing comparison into clear, verifiable components:
    * ** Agency & SaaS Pricing Models:** Contrasts hourly web dev (`US$100–$149/hr` in US/AU vs. `US$25–$49/hr` in PH via [Clutch Web Developers](https://clutch.co/web-developers/pricing) & [Clutch Web Designers](https://clutch.co/web-designers/pricing)), per-seat tools (`US$30/user/mo` via [Microsoft 365 Copilot](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/copilot/enterprise)), per-resolution bots (`US$0.99/outcome` via [Intercom](https://www.intercom.com/pricing) and `US$150/mo + $2.50/call` via [Smith.ai](https://smith.ai/pricing/ai-receptionist)), and retainers (`US$3,199/mo` avg via [Clutch SEO](https://clutch.co/seo-firms/pricing)) against Alliance's published fixed quotes and pass-through model costs.
    * **Full-Time Local Employer Costs vs. Alliance Embedded Developer:** Cites US `$196,500/yr` (`~$16,400/mo` via [BLS](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/computer-and-information-technology/software-developers.htm) & [BLS ECEC](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/ecec.t04.htm)), UK `£69,400/yr` (`~£5,800/mo` via [ONS](https://www.ons.gov.uk/employmentandlabourmarket/peopleinwork/earningsandworkinghours/bulletins/annualsurveyofhoursandearnings/2026) & [UK Gov](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/rates-and-thresholds-for-employers-2026-to-2027)), and Australia `A$147,800/yr` (`~A$12,300/mo` via [Jobs and Skills Australia](https://www.jobsandskills.gov.au/data/occupation-and-industry-profiles/occupations-anzsco/2613-software-and-applications-programmers) & [ATO](https://www.ato.gov.au/tax-rates-and-codes/key-superannuation-rates-and-thresholds/super-guarantee)), backed by [IBPAP (`$40.3B` industry)](https://admin.ibpap.org/storage/hub-resources/7Hv9U2uoLJVx2kxyRMObNm6W6L0MR57l9dZfrez7.pdf), [EF EPI 2025 (#2 in Asia)](https://www.ef.com/wwen/epi/regions/asia/philippines/), and [RA 10173](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/2/50253).
  * **Service Pillar Cards (Scale, Solve, Grow):** Visualized as three refracted output bands emerging from the **AI Check-Up** diagnostic card on the left—making Note 1's information architecture (*"lead with the three goals and let people open the full list from there"*) immediately intuitive.

## 5. Figma Best-Practices Verification Scorecard

* **App-Icon Scalability (`16×16px` & `60×60px`):** **PASS.** At `60×60px`, all three refracted output bars render with `2px` separation. At `16×16px` favicon scale, the three closely spaced right-hand bars simplify via responsive SVG breakpoint into a bold `2-bar` or flared wedge crossbar so the `16×16px` mark never suffers from sub-pixel crowding.
* **Monochrome (`1-Bit Black/White`) Test:** **PASS.** Because the single input bar on the left and the three refracted bars on the right are separated by `1.5px–2px` negative-space channels, the `1-into-3` prism geometry survives intact in 1-bit `#000000` on `#FFFFFF`.
* **Readability & Contrast Audit:** **PASS.** All three core brand colors pass **WCAG AAA (`> 7.0:1`)** on `#F9FAFC`: Monolith Basalt (`#16181D`) at `17.00:1`, Refracted Indigo-Cobalt (`#1E468A`) at `8.75:1`, and Refracted Emerald-Teal (`#19614B`) at `7.05:1`.
