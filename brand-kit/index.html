Colour

One palette, "A, P and the Ribbon": A for Alliance, P for Partner, and the ribbon between them. Navy and Daylight own the page; Alliance Blue is kept for the one action that matters.

Daybreak

Partner Navy to Alliance Blue (54 %) to Ribbon Cyan. Hero bands, the ribbon and brand graphics; never behind small text.

Shading

Eight tokens on two lightness ramps give the 3D mark its depth. Use them for depth in graphics, never as text colours on light grounds.

Ten lockups in six colour states. Use the full-colour mark wherever it can print; switch to flat or one-colour artwork when a process cannot hold a gradient.

Type

Mona Sans for every word, Mona Sans Mono for numbers that must line up. Its width axis lets headlines stand as wide as the wordmark while text stays easy to read.

Imagery

Everything is drawn from the logo kit: the ribbon, the facets of the mark and the swallow. Nature appears in the same language, never as stock photography of robots or glowing brains.

Icons

28 interface icons on a 24 px grid with a 1.75 stroke, five social glyphs, and twelve service icons from the logo kit.

Patterns

Three website patterns carry what makes Alliance different: how we work, prices you can check, and a team that works while you sleep. Live versions of every component are in the specimen.
