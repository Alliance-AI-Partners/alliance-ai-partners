# Concept 14: The Geodesic Dome / Hub — "Lessons Travel Across the Alliance; Partner Data Never Does."

## 1. Strategic Foundation & Logo Idea

* **Core Metaphor:** Directly visualizes Vision §2 (*"By 2035, we want to be a global hub for AI: the partner that companies around the world trust most to help them use AI and grow. That means: Hundreds of partner companies, each starting from what we've learned with the others. Lessons travel across the alliance; partner data never does"*) and Core Value #3 (**Trust holds everything up**—*"Partner data stays where the partner decides, and we never use it to train anything they don't own"*). In Buckminster Fuller's **Geodesic Structural Architecture**, triangulated space-frame struts distribute load evenly across an entire spherical vault while keeping each triangular bay structurally independent and sealed. Visually, we engineer a **5-Facet Triangulated Geodesic `A` Vault**: outer load-bearing struts in Titanium Geodesic Navy (`#101C29`) and Strut Bronze (`#7D481B`) connect across sealed, isolated triangular chambers in Node Teal (`#0D5C63`)—proving visually that shared architectural strength travels across the struts while each partner's interior vault remains completely walled off and private.
* **Master Mark Recommendation (Static vs. Dynamic):** **Hybrid Static-Vault + Dynamic-Lattice System.** The primary corporate mark uses a **Static 5-Facet Geodesic `A`** (strictly capped at 5 bold triangular/trapezoidal facets so it never resembles a complex circuit board or spiderweb). In interactive enterprise security diagrams, Private AI pages, and the 2035 Global Hub showcase (Type 12), the outer struts illuminate to show shared engineering playbooks traveling between nodes while the interior triangular vaults lock with a solid privacy shield.
* **Primary Sketch / Geometry Concept:**
  * **Base Construction Grid:** Engineered on a `24×24px` isometric grid (`240×240px` vector master) using `60°` and `30°` geodesic triangulation and a mandatory `2px` (`20pt`) negative-space strut-channel between all 5 facets.
  * **Five-Facet Geodesic `A` Topology (Anti-Clutter Rule):**
    1. **Top Crown Triangle (`#101C29`):** Forms the upper zenith of the `A` (`y = 2..9`).
    2. **Central Sealed Vault Triangle (`#0D5C63`):** An inverted equilateral triangle suspended at the center (`y = 9..16`) whose top horizontal edge forms the upper lintel and whose bottom vertex anchors the crossbar—symbolizing the **Sealed Partner Data Vault**.
    3. **Left & Right Upper Haunch Triangles (`#101C29`):** Flank the central vault (`y = 8..15`).
    4. **Left & Right Base Strut Trapezoids (`#7D481B` & `#101C29`):** Form the wide load-bearing legs (`y = 15..22`).

## 3. Design System & UI Integration Overview

* **Typography System:**
  * **Display / Headings:** **Space Grotesk** or **Plus Jakarta Sans** (`600 SemiBold`, `700 Bold`; Tracking: `-0.025em`; Line-Height: `1.14`). Faceted, architectural letterforms that mirror the triangulated space-frame struts of the geodesic vault.
  * **Body & UI Controls:** **Inter** (Weights: `400 Regular`, `500 Medium`, `600 SemiBold`; Base Size: `16px`; Line-Height: `1.6`). Plain-English clarity for explaining data isolation, security testing, and service outcomes.
  * **Monospace / Pricing & Data:** **IBM Plex Mono** (`500 Medium`). Used for security audit hashes, compliance citations ([Data Privacy Act of 2012 / RA 10173](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/2/50253), EU AI Act), published starting prices, and verified international salary tables.
* **Layout Grid & Spacing Tokens:**
  * **Desktop Grid:** `12-column` structural lattice grid (`1280px` max width, `24px` gutters) where card containers feature subtle `45°/60°` chamfered top-right corners (`8px` bevel) that echo the triangulated geodesic facets.
  * **Spacing Scale:** `8px` spatial unit (`8px`, `16px`, `24px`, `32px`, `48px`, `64px`, `96px`).
  * **Border & Radius Tokens:** `4px` structural radius (or `8px` chamfered polygon corners on featured security/governance cards) with `1px solid #C6D4E1` borders.
* **Key UI Component Guidelines:**
  * **Navigation & Logo Placement:** `72px` header on `#F7FAFC` featuring the **Geodesic `A` Combination Mark** on the left, main navigation items in the center, a compact *"Data Privacy Promise"* badge pill (`RA 10173 & EU AI Act Ready`), and the primary CTA button on the right.
  * **Honest Pricing Cards & Comparison Tables:** Designed as a "Transparent Alliance Ledger" on `#E9F0F5`. Contrasts traditional agency models—hourly rates (`US$100–$149/hr` in US/AU via [Clutch Web Developers](https://clutch.co/web-developers/pricing) & [Clutch Web Designers](https://clutch.co/web-designers/pricing)), per-seat stacking (`US$30/user/mo` via [Microsoft 365 Copilot](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/copilot/enterprise)), per-resolution fees (`US$0.99/outcome` via [Intercom](https://www.intercom.com/pricing) and `US$150/mo + $2.50/call` via [Smith.ai](https://smith.ai/pricing/ai-receptionist)), and retainers (`US$3,199/mo` avg via [Clutch SEO](https://clutch.co/seo-firms/pricing))—plus local full-time employer costs in the US (`~$196,500/yr` via [BLS](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/computer-and-information-technology/software-developers.htm) & [BLS ECEC](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/ecec.t04.htm)), UK (`~£69,400/yr` via [ONS](https://www.ons.gov.uk/employmentandlabourmarket/peopleinwork/earningsandworkinghours/bulletins/annualsurveyofhoursandearnings/2026) & [UK Gov](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/rates-and-thresholds-for-employers-2026-to-2027)), and Australia (`~A$147,800/yr` via [Jobs and Skills Australia](https://www.jobsandskills.gov.au/data/occupation-and-industry-profiles/occupations-anzsco/2613-software-and-applications-programmers) & [ATO](https://www.ato.gov.au/tax-rates-and-codes/key-superannuation-rates-and-thresholds/super-guarantee)) against Alliance's published fixed quotes, backed by the Philippines' `$40.3B` IT-BPM industry ([IBPAP](https://admin.ibpap.org/storage/hub-resources/7Hv9U2uoLJVx2kxyRMObNm6W6L0MR57l9dZfrez7.pdf)), #2 Asian English ranking ([EF EPI 2025](https://www.ef.com/wwen/epi/regions/asia/philippines/)), and independent National Privacy Commission enforcement ([Republic Act 10173](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/2/50253)).

## 5. Figma Best-Practices Verification Scorecard

* **App-Icon Scalability (`16×16px` & `60×60px`):** **PASS.** By strictly capping the geodesic triangulation at **5 bold facets** (1 crown, 1 central inverted triangle, 2 legs/haunches) separated by `2px` channels, the mark avoids the "busy wireframe mesh" trap and scales cleanly down to `16×16px` and `60×60px`.
* **Monochrome (`1-Bit Black/White`) Test:** **PASS.** Every triangular facet is separated from adjacent facets by a `2px` negative-space channel, so the triangulated `A` and central inverted vault remain 100% distinct in 1-bit `#000000` on `#FFFFFF`.
* **Readability & Contrast Audit:** **PASS.** All three core brand colors pass **WCAG AAA (`> 7.1:1`)** on `#F7FAFC`: Titanium Geodesic Navy (`#101C29`) at `16.42:1`, Sovereign Vault Teal (`#0D5C63`) at `7.34:1`, and Geodesic Strut Bronze (`#7D481B`) at `7.12:1`.
