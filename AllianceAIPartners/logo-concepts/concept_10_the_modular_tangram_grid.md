# Concept 10: The Modular Tangram Grid — "Understand. Plan. Build. Run and Grow."

**Brand Identity & Figma Logo System Specification · Alliance AI Partners (Brand DNA v2.1)**  
**Methodology Reference:** [Figma's 12 Types of Logos Framework](https://www.figma.com/resource-library/types-of-logos/)

## 1. Strategic Foundation & Logo Idea

* **Core Metaphor:** Directly visualizes Mission §3 (*"How we work with a new partner: 1. Understand -> 2. Plan -> 3. Build -> 4. Run and grow"*), Vision §2 (*"Our own products, built from problems we've solved many times over"*), and our modular service architecture (*"Partners can start with one service and add more over time"* across *Scale AI*, *Solve problems with AI*, and *Grow*). Just as an architectural **Tangram / Bauhaus Modular System** uses a fixed set of precision geometric building blocks (`45°` right triangles, a square, and a parallelogram) to construct any structure without wasted material, **Alliance AI Partners** assembles proven modular blocks (strategy, embedded engineers, Make/n8n/MCP automations, voice/chat agents, GEO/SEO growth) to fit each partner's exact problem. In its primary state, **4 core modular blocks** (representing *1. Understand, 2. Plan, 3. Build, 4. Run & Grow*) lock together across `2px` negative-space joints to form a razor-sharp architectural capital **`A`**.
* **Master Mark Recommendation (Static vs. Dynamic):** **Native Dynamic-Modular Identity with Static Master Lockup.** Among all 15 concepts, Concept 10 is the purest **Dynamic System** (Type 12) while maintaining a rock-solid **Static Master `A` Configuration** for legal, print, and header recognition. Because the surface area of the modular blocks never changes, the exact same geometric blocks that form the master **`A`** can rearrange on landing pages into a **3-Pillar Staircase (*Scale AI*)**, an **Interlocking Puzzle Square (*Solve problems with AI*)**, or an **Ascending Arrow (*Grow*)**.
* **Primary Sketch / Geometry Concept:**
  * **Base Construction Grid:** Engineered on a `24×24px` isometric/orthogonal Bauhaus grid (`240×240px` vector master) using `45°` and `90°` angles and a uniform `2px` (`20pt`) negative-space joint channel between all modular tiles.
  * **Four-Tile Master `A` Assembly (Favicon & Primary Icon):** To ensure `16×16px` simplicity (per Figma Principle 1), the master **`A`** uses exactly **4 geometric tiles** (rather than 7 tiny fragments):
    1. **Apex Triangle (`#141619`):** A `45°` isosceles right triangle crowning `y = 2..9`.
    2. **Left Parallelogram Pier (`#141619`):** Forms the angled left leg (`y = 9..22`).
    3. **Right Parallelogram Pier (`#1B4B9E`):** Forms the angled right leg (`y = 9..22`).
    4. **Central Square/Trapezoid Crossbar Core (`#16654E`):** Locks horizontally between the two piers at `y = 11..16`.
  * **Full 7-Tile Tangram Extension:** Reserved for large display graphics (`>=120px`), animations (Type 10), and service-architecture diagrams where the 7 tiles map to our 11 industry solution packages and 3 service goals.

## 3. Design System & UI Integration Overview

* **Typography System:**
  * **Display / Headings:** **Plus Jakarta Sans** or **Space Grotesk** (Weights: `600 SemiBold`, `700 Bold`; Tracking: `-0.03em`; Line-Height: `1.12`). Geometric letterforms built from modular circles, triangles, and squares that echo the 4-tile Tangram `A`.
  * **Body & UI Controls:** **Inter** (Weights: `400 Regular`, `500 Medium`, `600 SemiBold`; Base Size: `16px`; Line-Height: `1.58`). Clean, neutral, and effortless to scan across modular cards.
  * **Monospace / Pricing & Data:** **JetBrains Mono** (`500 Medium`, `600 SemiBold`). Used for module tags (`MOD-01 · AI CHECK-UP`, `MOD-02 · EMBEDDED DEV`), published starting prices, and verified international salary tables.
* **Layout Grid & Spacing Tokens:**
  * **Desktop Grid:** `12-column` **Modular Bento & Tangram Grid** (`1280px` max width, `16px` uniform joint gutters—matching the `2px` negative-space joint philosophy of the logo). Every page section is composed of modular cards (`4×4`, `4×8`, `6×6`, `12×4`) that lock into a complete rectangle without ragged bottom edges.
  * **Spacing Scale:** `8px` spatial module (`8px`, `16px`, `24px`, `32px`, `48px`, `64px`, `96px`).
  * **Border & Radius Tokens:** `4px` uniform tile radius (`2px` internal badges) with `1px solid #D4D8DF` borders.
* **Key UI Component Guidelines:**
  * **Navigation & Logo Placement:** `72px` modular top bar where the **4-Tile Tangram `A` Combination Mark** sits in the left module, a segmented pill bar (*Scale AI* | *Solve Problems* | *Grow* | *Industry Packages* | *Honest Pricing*) sits in the center module, and the primary CTA button sits in the right module.
  * **Honest Pricing Cards & Comparison Tables:** Designed as an interactive **"Build Your Partnership Stack"** modular configurator on `#F0F1F3`. Users can inspect published starting prices for individual modules (*Websites*, *The AI check-up*, *AI automation projects*, *Embedded developers*) and compare them directly against traditional pricing traps—hourly billing (`US$100–$149/hr` via [Clutch Web Developers](https://clutch.co/web-developers/pricing) & [Clutch Web Designers](https://clutch.co/web-designers/pricing)), per-seat licenses (`US$30/user/mo` via [Microsoft 365 Copilot](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/copilot/enterprise)), per-resolution fees (`US$0.99/outcome` via [Intercom](https://www.intercom.com/pricing) and `US$150/mo + $2.50/call` via [Smith.ai](https://smith.ai/pricing/ai-receptionist)), and opaque retainers (`US$3,199/mo` avg via [Clutch SEO](https://clutch.co/seo-firms/pricing))—as well as local full-time employer costs in the US (`$196,500/yr` via [BLS](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/computer-and-information-technology/software-developers.htm) & [BLS ECEC](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/ecec.t04.htm)), UK (`£69,400/yr` via [ONS](https://www.ons.gov.uk/employmentandlabourmarket/peopleinwork/earningsandworkinghours/bulletins/annualsurveyofhoursandearnings/2026) & [UK Gov](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/rates-and-thresholds-for-employers-2026-to-2027)), and Australia (`A$147,800/yr` via [Jobs and Skills Australia](https://www.jobsandskills.gov.au/data/occupation-and-industry-profiles/occupations-anzsco/2613-software-and-applications-programmers) & [ATO](https://www.ato.gov.au/tax-rates-and-codes/key-superannuation-rates-and-thresholds/super-guarantee)), backed by [IBPAP](https://admin.ibpap.org/storage/hub-resources/7Hv9U2uoLJVx2kxyRMObNm6W6L0MR57l9dZfrez7.pdf), [EF EPI 2025](https://www.ef.com/wwen/epi/regions/asia/philippines/), and [RA 10173](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/2/50253).

## 5. Figma Best-Practices Verification Scorecard

* **App-Icon Scalability (`16×16px` & `60×60px`):** **PASS.** By engineering the master `A` from **4 bold tiles** (rather than 7 small pieces) separated by `2px` (`1.5px` at `16×16px`) orthogonal and `45°` seams, the mark remains super-crisp at `16×16px` and `60×60px`.
* **Monochrome (`1-Bit Black/White`) Test:** **PASS.** Every tile is physically separated from its neighbors by a continuous `2px` negative-space seam, so the 4-block modular construction reads unmistakably in 1-bit `#000000` on `#FFFFFF` and inverted `#FFFFFF` on `#000000`.
* **Readability & Contrast Audit:** **PASS.** Swiss Carbon Black (`#141619` on `#FAFAFA`) achieves `17.37:1` (WCAG AAA), Bauhaus Engineering Cobalt (`#1B4B9E` on `#FAFAFA`) achieves `7.90:1` (WCAG AAA), and Tangram Jade Green (`#16654E` on `#FAFAFA`) achieves `6.69:1` (WCAG AA).
