## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js on Vercel (the user's choice, 2026-11-02). The design system ships CSS custom properties plus a Tailwind preset that a Next.js app can load.

## Users

Founders and CEOs of companies in the United States, Australia, the United Kingdom and other countries. They know AI could help their business, but their teams are buried in slow, manual work and have neither the time nor the skills to make it happen. On the website they are deciding whether Alliance AI Partners can be the AI team they don't have to hire: they compare it with hiring locally, with US and Australian agencies, and with per-seat AI tools.

Alliance AI Partners calls the companies it works with "partners"; "customers" means the partners' own customers.

## Product Purpose

Alliance AI Partners is an AI company based in the Philippines. It teams up with companies abroad to scale AI, solve business problems with AI, and grow, working as part of each partner's team. It exists to close the gap between knowing AI could help and having the people to make it happen, so companies get more done and grow without wearing out their people.

Success on the website: a founder starts an "AI check-up", the short, low-risk first project where Alliance finds where AI could save the partner time or money (primary call to action: "See where AI can help").

## Positioning

- An embedded team in the Philippines that works inside the partner's team, and stays: "We build it, run it, and help you grow." Most agencies hand over the project and move on.
- It costs less for two stated reasons, said with pride: AI does the repetitive work, so projects need fewer billable hours, and the team's costs are Philippine costs, passed on to partners.
- Honest pricing a partner can check: published starting prices for every core service, a fixed quote before work starts, no per-seat fees on tools it builds, AI usage passed on at cost and shown on the invoice, monthly plans cancellable with [30] days' notice, and the partner owns what is built.
- It says so when AI, or Alliance, is not the right answer.

## Operating Context

- Engagement in four steps: Understand, Plan, Build, Run and grow. A fix may be AI or automation, better design, extra developers or a marketing push, and sometimes involves no AI at all.
- First sales calls start by listening; proposals open with what changes for the reader and carry a fixed quote.
- Time zones are part of the offer: Sydney is two hours ahead (three in Australian daylight saving); US partners brief before logging off and see progress when they log on; the UK morning is the Philippine afternoon.
- Partners see progress every week, a person reviews every piece of work, and partner data stays in the partner's systems wherever possible. The Philippines' Data Privacy Act of 2012 is enforced by an independent National Privacy Commission.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Services under three goals:
  - Scale AI: strategy and leadership, training and adoption, software and product engineering, running AI for you, robotics and physical AI, talent and teams.
  - Solve problems with AI: automation and AI agents, customer support, knowledge and data, design, security and risk.
  - Grow: marketing, sales and go-to-market, online stores. Ready-made packages exist for common industries.
- Price claims follow the Brand DNA rules: every number has a source and a date, comparisons are like for like and proven in all three markets (FTC, UK ASA, ACCC), and never "cheapest", "best rate" or "a fraction of the cost".
- Starting prices are still placeholders ([PRICE]) and must be filled with real figures before launch.
- "The Alliance AI platform" may be mentioned only once that platform exists.
- Open: the site map and page list, the CMS or content source, analytics, and the domain.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: always the full "Alliance AI Partners"; "AI Partners" alone sounds like any AI firm.
- Personality: the calm expert in the room. Sage first (the wise guide who tells the truth), Ruler second (owns the outcome); never the Magician (AI "magic") or the Creator. Confident, clear, forward-looking, calm and loyal; never arrogant, hard to follow, overhyped, cold or afraid to push back.
- Writing: start with the result, listen first, be clear and specific. Short sentences, everyday words, one real number over a string of adjectives; "we will", not "we can".
- Words to use: partner, team, together, build, connect, simple, clear, proven, results, grow. Words never to use: leverage, seamless, cutting-edge, revolutionary, game-changing, disrupt, supercharge, unleash, unlock, 10x, synergy, magic, ninja, guru, rockstar, hustle, alpha.
- Taglines (Brand DNA section 7), including "Put AI to work." for the homepage and "Partners in AI. Partners in growth." for the lockup and email signature.
- Finalized identity assets: the 3D A+P ribbon mark, the Montserrat Bold wordmark, the unified palette "A, P and the Ribbon", the brand graphics and the swallow mascot (see Evidence on Hand).
- Skip the usual AI look: no sparkles, glowing brains, circuit boards, robot faces, or purple-and-pink gradients.
- The visual world comes only from the logo kit: the A+P ribbon mark, its palette and shading, and the brand graphics (wave, facets, swallow). No Philippine or other regional cultural motifs, because Alliance works internationally (user, 2026-11-02). The Philippines base stays a stated fact in copy, never a visual theme.

## Product Principles

1. Lead with the result. Every page opens with what changes for the reader; technology appears only when it helps a decision.
2. Make every claim checkable. Prices are published, numbers are sourced and dated, and a promise appears only once it is true.
3. Simple on purpose. The simplest thing that does the job, written down clearly enough for a new hire to follow.
4. Trust holds everything up. Partner data stays where the partner decides, and bad news travels early with a plan.
5. Partnership over handoff. Alliance joins the partner's team and stays to run and grow what it built.
