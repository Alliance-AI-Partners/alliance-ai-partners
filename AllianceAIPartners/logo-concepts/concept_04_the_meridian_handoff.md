## 1. Strategic Foundation & Logo Idea

* **Core Metaphor:** Anchored directly in Vision §2 (*"By 2035, we want to be a global hub for AI"*), Elevator Pitch §6 (*"The Philippines pitch"*), and Taglines §7 (*"Two hours behind Sydney. Nowhere near Sydney prices."* and *"Brief us before you log off. See progress when you log on."*). Visually, the mark combines two intersecting architectural geometry systems: a sharp, ascending meridian peak (forming the outer legs of a capital **`A`**) and a crisp orbital horizon arc that passes directly through the `A` as its crossbar—linking the **121°E Manila Meridian** with **Sydney** (`+2h/+3h` per [NSW Government](https://www.nsw.gov.au/about-nsw/daylight-saving)), **London** (`-7h/-8h`), and **New York / San Francisco** (`-12h to -16h` per [NIST](https://www.nist.gov/pml/time-and-frequency-division/local-time-faqs)). The intersection of the solar horizon arc and the meridian peak represents the seamless **24-Hour Global Handoff**: while US and UK partners rest—or while Australian partners work in real-time daytime overlap—our Philippines team drives the project forward.
* **Master Mark Recommendation (Static vs. Dynamic):** **Hybrid Static-Meridian + Dynamic Time-Zone System.** The primary corporate mark uses a **Static Geometric Lockup** where the Manila-to-World orbital arc forms the permanent crossbar of the **`A`**. In digital products, regional landing pages (`/us`, `/au`, `/uk`), and partner portals, the mark operates as a **Dynamic Time-Zone & Handoff System** (Type 12): the node along the horizon crossbar shifts position and color between **Manila Dawn Ochre (`#9C4416`)** and **Sydney/Pacific Tasman Teal (`#0F5E6B`)** to reflect live operating overlap with the viewer's local time zone.
* **Primary Sketch / Geometry Concept:**
  * **Base Construction Grid:** Constructed on a `24×24px` square grid (`240×240px` master artboard) combining straight architectural meridian struts (`64°` base angle) with a precision circular horizon arc (`r = 16px`, center at `x = 12, y = 26`).
  * **Meridian Peak (`A` Frame):** Two clean, tapering vector struts rise from `x = 4, y = 21` and `x = 20, y = 21` to meet at a flat, chiseled `3px`-wide zenith cap (`x = 10.5..13.5, y = 3`).
  * **Orbital Horizon Crossbar:** Instead of a flat static bar, a crisp convex horizon arc (`2.5px` stroke weight, separated from the left leg by a `2px` negative-space handoff notch) sweeps from `x = 2, y = 15` across the interior of the `A` to `x = 22, y = 13`, anchored by a solid `3.5px` circular **Manila Hub Node** in Manila Dawn Ochre (`#9C4416`) at the eastern intersection.
  * **Small-Size Rules:** Below `24×24px`, the horizon arc thickens by `20%` and the negative-space notch locks at `1.5px` so the crossbar arc and meridian legs remain sharp at `16×16px`.

## 3. Design System & UI Integration Overview

  * **Body & UI Controls:** **Inter** (Weights: `400 Regular`, `500 Medium`, `600 SemiBold`; Base Size: `16px`; Line-Height: `1.6`). Ensures crystal-clear communication across US, UK, and Australian English conventions.
  * **Monospace / Pricing & Data:** **JetBrains Mono** (`500 Medium`, `600 SemiBold`). Used for UTC time-zone offsets (`MNL UTC+8`, `SYD UTC+10/+11`, `LON UTC+0/+1`, `NYC UTC-5/-4`), published starting prices, and FTC/ASA/ACCC-substantiated cost comparisons.

## 5. Figma Best-Practices Verification Scorecard

* **App-Icon Scalability (`16×16px` & `60×60px`):** **PASS.** At `60×60px`, the meridian `A`, horizon arc, and `3.5px` ochre node form an unmistakable icon. At `16×16px`, the node enlarges slightly to `3px` with a `1.5px` negative-space halo so the meridian `A` and sunrise dot remain crisp and readable at browser-tab scale.
* **Monochrome (`1-Bit Black/White`) Test:** **PASS.** Because the circular handoff node is separated from the meridian leg and horizon arc by a continuous `2px` negative-space ring, the three geometric components (meridian legs, curved horizon crossbar, and circular node) remain completely distinct in 1-bit `#000000` on `#FFFFFF` and inverted `#FFFFFF` on `#000000`.
* **Readability & Contrast Audit:** **PASS.** Pacific Midnight Navy (`#0E1B2E` on `#F8FAFC`) achieves `16.52:1` (WCAG AAA), Manila Dawn Ochre (`#9C4416` on `#F8FAFC`) achieves `6.17:1` (WCAG AA), and Tasman Meridian Teal (`#0F5E6B` on `#F8FAFC`) achieves `7.08:1` (WCAG AAA).
