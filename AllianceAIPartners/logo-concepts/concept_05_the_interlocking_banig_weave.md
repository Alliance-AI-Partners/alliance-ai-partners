# Concept 5: The Interlocking Banig Weave — "One Team, One Goal"

**Brand Identity & Figma Logo System Specification · Alliance AI Partners (Brand DNA v2.1)**  
**Methodology Reference:** [Figma's 12 Types of Logos Framework](https://www.figma.com/resource-library/types-of-logos/)

## 1. Strategic Foundation & Logo Idea

* **Core Metaphor:** Rooted in Core Value #1 (**One team, one goal**—*"When we partner with a company, their goals become our goals. We work as part of their team"*), Note 2 (*"Pitch outsourcing as a partnership: embedded developers who join the partner's team, with all of Alliance behind them"*), and proud Philippine craftsmanship. Across the Philippines, the **Banig** (handwoven pandan, buri, or tikog mat) and architectural rattan/bamboo lattice joinery gain extraordinary tensile strength because two perpendicular strands weave *over and under* one another—neither strand is strong alone, but woven together they cannot be pulled apart. We translate this heritage into a disciplined **Swiss Modernist Geometric Weave**: two angled structural ribbons—one in Pandan Deep Forest (`#195E3A`, the partner's team) and one in Abaca Charcoal (`#1C211E`, the Alliance PH team)—interlock at the crown and crossbar in a crisp over-under diamond weave (`45°/60°`) to form a monumental capital **`A`**, locked at the center by a Buri Woven Rust (`#8F3A22`) knot tile.
* **Master Mark Recommendation (Static vs. Dynamic):** **Hybrid Static-Knot + Dynamic-Lattice System.** The primary corporate mark uses a **Static 3-Ribbon Interlocking `A`** that stays consistent across all primary brand touchpoints. For team staffing pages (*Embedded developers*, *Dedicated AI teams*), multi-agent systems (*Teams of AI agents*), and industry verticals, the mark extends into a **Dynamic Weave System** (Type 12) where additional modular strands weave horizontally or diagonally into the master lattice without breaking the `2px` negative-space over-under gap rules.
* **Primary Sketch / Geometry Concept:**
  * **Base Construction Grid:** Built on a `24×24px` isometric/diamond weave grid (`240×240px` vector master) using `4px`-wide flat geometric ribbons separated at every over-under crossing by a mandatory `2px` (`20pt`) negative-space shadow gap.
  * **Over-Under Interlock (`A` Topology):**
    * **Strand 1 (Left-to-Apex-to-Crossbar):** Rises from the bottom-left (`x = 3, y = 21`) at `60°` to the apex (`x = 12, y = 3`), folds over at `60°`, and weaves *under* the right leg to form the horizontal crossbar.
    * **Strand 2 (Right-to-Crossbar-to-Apex):** Rises from the bottom-right (`x = 21, y = 21`), passes *over* the crossbar junction, and locks into the left shoulder at the apex.
    * **Central Diamond Lock:** The negative space inside the upper triangle and the overlapping crossbar junction form two concentric `60°` woven diamonds—a classic Philippine *binakol / banig* geometric motif—executed with Bauhaus/Pentagram mathematical restraint.

## 3. Design System & UI Integration Overview

* **Typography System:**
  * **Display / Headings:** **Bricolage Grotesque** or **Plus Jakarta Sans** (Weights: `600 SemiBold`, `700 Bold`; Tracking: `-0.02em`; Line-Height: `1.15`). Combines warm humanist ink-traps (reminiscent of interlocking woven joints) with crisp modernist proportions—projecting a team that is both deeply technical and genuinely human.
  * **Body & UI Controls:** **Inter** (Weights: `400 Regular`, `500 Medium`, `600 SemiBold`; Base Size: `16px`; Line-Height: `1.6`). Plain-spoken, clear, and accessible across all partner communications.
  * **Monospace / Pricing & Data:** **JetBrains Mono** (`500 Medium`). Used for transparent staffing rates, cost-comparison breakdowns, and Make/n8n/Zapier/MCP integration specs.
* **Layout Grid & Spacing Tokens:**
  * **Desktop Grid:** `12-column` interlocking bento grid (`1280px` max width, `20px` gutters) where adjacent cards alternate proportions (`7-col + 5-col` on Row 1, `5-col + 7-col` on Row 2) like a woven ashlar/banig pattern, visually reinforcing interlocking teamwork.
  * **Spacing Scale:** `8px` spatial rhythm (`8px`, `16px`, `24px`, `32px`, `48px`, `64px`, `96px`).
  * **Border & Radius Tokens:** `6px` card radius with `1px solid #D6D0C2` borders and signature `45°` corner weave ticks on featured callout containers.
* **Key UI Component Guidelines:**
  * **Navigation & Logo Placement:** `72px` header on `#FAF9F5` featuring the **Interlocking Banig `A` Combination Mark** on the left, clean navigation links in the middle, and a dual-action right rail: *"Honest Pricing"* secondary link + *"See where AI can help"* primary button.
  * **Honest Pricing Cards & Comparison Tables:** Designed around the "Embedded Team vs. Local Hiring" story. Features side-by-side cards showing how an Alliance embedded developer weaves directly into a US (`$196,500/yr` employer cost via [BLS May 2025](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/computer-and-information-technology/software-developers.htm) and [BLS June 2026](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/ecec.t04.htm)), UK (`£69,400/yr` via [ONS 2026](https://www.ons.gov.uk/employmentandlabourmarket/peopleinwork/earningsandworkinghours/bulletins/annualsurveyofhoursandearnings/2026) and [UK Gov 2026–27](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/rates-and-thresholds-for-employers-2026-to-2027)), or Australian (`A$147,800/yr` via [Jobs and Skills Australia](https://www.jobsandskills.gov.au/data/occupation-and-industry-profiles/occupations-anzsco/2613-software-and-applications-programmers) and [ATO](https://www.ato.gov.au/tax-rates-and-codes/key-superannuation-rates-and-thresholds/super-guarantee)) team at roughly half the local cost—backed by the Philippines' `$40.3B` IT-BPM ecosystem ([IBPAP](https://admin.ibpap.org/storage/hub-resources/7Hv9U2uoLJVx2kxyRMObNm6W6L0MR57l9dZfrez7.pdf)), "High" English proficiency ([EF EPI 2025](https://www.ef.com/wwen/epi/regions/asia/philippines/)), and strict data protection ([Republic Act 10173](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/2/50253)) where *"Lessons travel across the alliance; partner data never does."*
  * **Service Pillar Cards (Scale, Solve, Grow):** Three interlocking Bento Cards where each pillar card displays a distinct woven header ribbon: *Scale AI* in Abaca Charcoal (`#1C211E`), *Solve problems with AI* in Pandan Deep Forest (`#195E3A`), and *Grow* in Buri Woven Rust (`#8F3A22`).
  * **Buttons & Interactive States:** Primary CTA (*"See where AI can help"*) uses Pandan Deep Forest (`#195E3A`) with `#FAF9F5` text (`7.37:1` AAA) and a `4px` radius. **Hover:** Shifts to `#12452A` (`9.85:1` AAA). **Focus:** `2px` solid `#8F3A22` outer ring with `2px` `#FAF9F5` offset (`7.13:1` AAA).

## 5. Figma Best-Practices Verification Scorecard

* **App-Icon Scalability (`16×16px` & `60×60px`):** **PASS.** Weave logos often fail at small sizes when they use too many thin threads. By restricting the mark to **only two bold `4px` ribbons** and **two `2px` over-under negative-space cuts** (`1.5px` at `16×16px`), the Woven-Ribbon `A` remains bold, open, and readable down to a `16×16px` favicon.
* **Monochrome (`1-Bit Black/White`) Test:** **PASS.** The over-under weave illusion relies entirely on the `2px` negative-space gap cut into the lower ribbon at each crossing—the exact technique used in classic monochrome Bauhaus and Japanese *mon* crests. In pure `#000000` on `#FFFFFF` or inverted `#FFFFFF` on `#000000`, the over-under depth reads instantaneously.
* **Readability & Contrast Audit:** **PASS.** All three primary colors achieve **WCAG AAA (`> 7.0:1`)** against the light surface (`#FAF9F5`): Abaca Charcoal (`#1C211E`) at `15.50:1`, Pandan Deep Forest (`#195E3A`) at `7.37:1`, and Buri Woven Rust (`#8F3A22`) at `7.13:1`.
