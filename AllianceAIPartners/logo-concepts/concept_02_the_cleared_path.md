# Concept 2: The Cleared Path — "Less Busywork. More Growth."

**Brand Identity & Figma Logo System Specification · Alliance AI Partners (Brand DNA v2.1)**  
**Methodology Reference:** [Figma's 12 Types of Logos Framework](https://www.figma.com/resource-library/types-of-logos/)

## 1. Strategic Foundation & Logo Idea

* **Core Metaphor:** Built directly from Purpose §1 (*"Many companies know AI could help them, but their teams are buried in slow, manual work... We exist to close that gap"*), Core Value #2 (**Simple on purpose**), and Core Value #4 (**Clear the path**—*"We go first. We try new AI tools on our own business before we bring them to partners"*). Visually, two fragmented, parallel tracks on the left—representing a partner's bottlenecked manual operations and Alliance AI Partners' battle-tested workflows—converge seamlessly at a `60°` architectural switch-point into a single wide, unobstructed forward highway rising toward the upper right (`A` silhouette). The negative-space corridor cutting cleanly through the center is **The Cleared Path**: the frictionless lane where repetitive busywork disappears and teams move forward without burning out.
* **Master Mark Recommendation (Static vs. Dynamic):** **Hybrid Static-Anchor + Dynamic-Wayfinding System.** The core corporate mark uses a **Static Geometric Anchor** where the outer boundary forms a forward-leaning architectural **`A`** cut through its center by a crisp negative-space highway. Across digital interfaces, documentation portals, and workflow dashboards, the mark extends into a **Dynamic Wayfinding System** (Type 12): the central cleared corridor acts as a live status track indicating progress across our 4-step partner methodology (*1. Understand -> 2. Plan -> 3. Build -> 4. Run and grow*).
* **Primary Sketch / Geometry Concept:**
  * **Base Construction Grid:** Engineered on a `24×24px` square grid (`240×240px` master artboard) built on `60°` isometric/equilateral angles and a `2px` (`20pt`) spatial module.
  * **Left & Right Track Ribbons:** Two parallel ascending vector ribbons (`4px` wide) rise from the lower-left (`x = 3, y = 21`) and lower-right (`x = 21, y = 21`) at `60°` angles. At `y = 13`, an internal horizontal switch-bridge connects them while a central `3px`-wide negative-space channel cuts straight upward through the apex (`x = 12, y = 2`), dividing the crown into twin synchronized directional arrowheads.
  * **Negative-Space Highway:** The central vertical-to-angled negative-space lane widens smoothly from `2px` at the apex to `6px` at the base, creating strong optical perspective—like looking down a freshly cleared runway or express lane.
  * **Stroke & Corner Rules:** All Ribbon terminals are cut flush at `0°` horizontal or `60°` diagonal angles with `0.5px` micro-chamfers; zero rounded blobs or organic curves.

## 3. Design System & UI Integration Overview

* **Typography System:**
  * **Display / Headings:** **Space Grotesk** or **Sora** (Weights: `600 SemiBold`, `700 Bold`; Tracking: `-0.03em`; Line-Height: `1.12`). Inspired by precision airport and highway wayfinding typography—engineered so a busy founder scanning a page can read the outcome headline (*"Put AI to work."*) in under one second.
  * **Body & UI Controls:** **Public Sans** (Weights: `400 Regular`, `500 Medium`, `600 SemiBold`; Base Size: `16px`; Line-Height: `1.55`). Developed for high-trust public-interest clarity; embodies Brand DNA Rule #3 (*"Use short sentences and everyday words. One real number does more than a string of adjectives"*).
  * **Monospace / Pricing & Data:** **IBM Plex Mono** (Weights: `500 Medium`, `600 SemiBold`). Used for workflow step numbers (`01 UNDERSTAND`, `02 PLAN`, `03 BUILD`, `04 RUN & GROW`), automation latency/hours-saved counters, and fixed-quote tables.
* **Layout Grid & Spacing Tokens:**
  * **Desktop Grid:** `12-column` asymmetrical wayfinding grid (`1280px` max width, `24px` gutters) featuring a dedicated `3-column` left-hand "Wayfinding Rail" that stays pinned during scroll, showing the reader exactly where they are on the page.
  * **Spacing Scale:** Generous open-corridor spacing built on an `8px` base unit (`8px`, `16px`, `24px`, `32px`, `48px`, `80px`, `120px`), enforcing at least `35%` open background canvas (`#F9FAFB`) on every viewport so interfaces never feel crowded.
  * **Border & Radius Tokens:** `6px` outer card radius, `4px` button radius, and `1.5px` directional connector lines with `60°` mitered corners.
* **Key UI Component Guidelines:**
  * **Navigation & Logo Placement:** Top navigation bar (`68px` height) styled like an international transit/wayfinding header: the **Combination Mark** sits on the far left, followed by numbered section links (`01 Scale AI`, `02 Solve Problems`, `03 Grow`, `04 Honest Pricing`) separated by `60°` forward slashes (`/`), and the high-contrast `#0051A8` CTA button on the far right.
  * **Honest Pricing Cards & Comparison Tables:** Designed as "Direct Route vs. Detour" comparison cards. Each card contrasts how other firms charge—hourly billing at `US$100–$149/hr` ([Clutch Web Developers](https://clutch.co/web-developers/pricing)), per-seat stacking like `US$30/user/mo` ([Microsoft 365 Copilot](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/copilot/enterprise)), per-resolution fees like `US$0.99/outcome` ([Intercom](https://www.intercom.com/pricing)) or `US$2.50/call` ([Smith.ai](https://smith.ai/pricing/ai-receptionist)), and opaque retainers averaging `US$3,199/mo` ([Clutch SEO](https://clutch.co/seo-firms/pricing))—against Alliance AI Partners' single cleared path: published starting prices, fixed project quotes, model usage passed on at cost, and embedded developers at roughly half the cost of hiring locally in the US (`$196,500/yr` employer cost per [BLS](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/computer-and-information-technology/software-developers.htm) and [BLS ECEC](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/ecec.t04.htm)), UK (`£69,400/yr` per [ONS](https://www.ons.gov.uk/employmentandlabourmarket/peopleinwork/earningsandworkinghours/bulletins/annualsurveyofhoursandearnings/2026) and [UK Gov](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/rates-and-thresholds-for-employers-2026-to-2027)), or Australia (`A$147,800/yr` per [Jobs and Skills Australia](https://www.jobsandskills.gov.au/data/occupation-and-industry-profiles/occupations-anzsco/2613-software-and-applications-programmers) and [ATO](https://www.ato.gov.au/tax-rates-and-codes/key-superannuation-rates-and-thresholds/super-guarantee)).
  * **Service Pillar Cards (Scale, Solve, Grow):** Horizontal "Three-Lane Highway" selector. Selecting a lane highlights its track in `#0051A8` (*Scale AI*), `#111C24` (*Solve problems with AI*), or `#146B48` (*Grow*), revealing a clean 3-column grid of plain-English service cards (e.g., *AI check-up*, *AI connectors (MCP)*, *AI search visibility (GEO)*).
  * **Buttons & Interactive States:** Primary CTA (*"See where AI can help"*) uses Signal Wayfinder Blue (`#0051A8`) with `#F9FAFB` text (`7.33:1` AAA) and a trailing `60°` forward chevron icon (`->`) that translates `4px` right on hover (`cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`). **Focus State:** `2px` solid `#111C24` outer focus ring with `2px` `#F9FAFB` offset (`16.53:1`).

## 5. Figma Best-Practices Verification Scorecard

* **App-Icon Scalability (`16×16px` & `60×60px`):** **PASS.** At `60×60px`, the Split-Corridor `A` reads with razor-sharp highway perspective. At `16×16px`, the secondary internal switch-notch simplifies into a single bold two-into-one chevron `A` with a `2px` central negative-space slit, maintaining instant legibility across browser tabs and mobile home screens.
* **Monochrome (`1-Bit Black/White`) Test:** **PASS.** The `2px` negative-space channel separates the left track from the right merging highway along its entire length, ensuring the two-into-one merge reads unmistakably in pure `#000000` on `#FFFFFF` and inverted `#FFFFFF` on `#000000`.
* **Readability & Contrast Audit:** **PASS.** Obsidian Pine Ink (`#111C24` on `#F9FAFB`) achieves `16.53:1` (WCAG AAA), Signal Wayfinder Blue (`#0051A8` on `#F9FAFB`) achieves `7.33:1` (WCAG AAA), and Clear-Path Emerald (`#146B48` on `#F9FAFB`) achieves `6.23:1` (WCAG AA).
