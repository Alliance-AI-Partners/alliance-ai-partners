# Concept 8: The Dovetail Joint — "Built Inside Your Team, Locked for the Long Run"

**Brand Identity & Figma Logo System Specification · Alliance AI Partners (Brand DNA v2.1)**  
**Methodology Reference:** [Figma's 12 Types of Logos Framework](https://www.figma.com/resource-library/types-of-logos/)

## 1. Strategic Foundation & Logo Idea

* **Core Metaphor:** Grounded in *Who We Are* (*"We call these companies our partners, because we work as part of their team"*), Elevator Pitch §6 (*"Where most agencies hand over the project and move on, we stay to keep everything running and help you win more customers"*), and Core Value #1 (**One team, one goal**). In master woodworking, timber framing, and precision mechanical engineering, the **Sliding Dovetail Joint** (composed of a flared trapezoidal **Tail** and a matching recessed **Socket/Pin**) is the strongest way to join two separate members into a single load-bearing piece without nails, screws, or fragile glue. Pulling on a dovetail joint only locks it tighter. Visually, the upper element (the Partner's business) and the lower-embedded element (Alliance AI Partners' team) interlock via a crisp `76°` flared dovetail crossbar inside the silhouette of a monumental capital **`A`**—proving that our embedded developers, AI agents, and support systems fit flush inside the partner's operation.
* **Master Mark Recommendation (Static vs. Dynamic):** **Hybrid Static-Joinery + Dynamic-Fit System.** The primary corporate mark uses a **Static Two-Piece Dovetail `A`** where the upper pin block (`#211814`) and lower flared tail wedge (`#824616`) lock across a uniform `2px` negative-space kerf line. In interactive product tours, service configurators, and dynamic headers (Type 12), the two halves slide together from different angles (vertical drop, horizontal slide, or `60°` miter lock) to visualize how each service (*Scale AI*, *Solve problems with AI*, *Grow*) slots into an existing tech stack.
* **Primary Sketch / Geometry Concept:**
  * **Base Construction Grid:** Engineered on a `24×24px` master grid (`240×240px` vector artboard) governed by the classic `1:6` (`~80.5°`) to `1:4` (`~76°`) hardwood dovetail slope angle and a `2px` (`20pt`) negative-space saw-kerf channel.
  * **Upper Pin Hood (`A` Crown):** A solid flat-topped trapezoidal `A` hood in Narra Hardwood Espresso (`#211814`) spans from `y = 2` to `y = 14`, with its central bottom edge carved upward into a recessed inverted-trapezoid **Dovetail Socket**.
  * **Lower Tail Wedge & Legs:** Rising from `y = 22` to `y = 9`, the lower structural component in Brass Gauge Ochre (`#824616`) and Workshop Slate (`#235347`) forms the two lower base legs of the `A` and projects a flared trapezoidal **Dovetail Tenon** straight up into the upper socket across a `2px` negative-space kerf—so the negative-space kerf line itself traces a dramatic flared dovetail profile across the waist of the `A`.

## 3. Design System & UI Integration Overview

* **Typography System:**
  * **Display / Headings:** **Bricolage Grotesque** or **Space Grotesk** (Weights: `600 SemiBold`, `700 Bold`; Tracking: `-0.025em`; Line-Height: `1.14`). Features crisp, flared ink-traps at every stem junction that visually echo the `76°` dovetail joinery of the logo mark.
  * **Body & UI Controls:** **Public Sans** or **Inter** (Weights: `400 Regular`, `500 Medium`, `600 SemiBold`; Base Size: `16px`; Line-Height: `1.6`). Delivers plain-spoken, zero-hype readability.
  * **Monospace / Pricing & Data:** **JetBrains Mono** (`500 Medium`). Used for joinery tolerances, published starting prices, fixed quotes, and US/UK/AU employer cost comparisons.
* **Layout Grid & Spacing Tokens:**
  * **Desktop Grid:** `12-column` interlocking joinery grid (`1280px` max width, `24px` gutters) where two-column comparison blocks (*What the Partner Brings* vs. *What Alliance Builds & Runs*, or *Before* vs. *After*) meet along a notched dovetail divider line in the center gutter.
  * **Spacing Scale:** `8px` modular grid (`8px`, `16px`, `24px`, `32px`, `48px`, `64px`, `96px`).
  * **Border & Radius Tokens:** `3px` crisp planed corners with `1px solid #D5CBBF` borders and signature `76°` flared dovetail tabs on active tab-bars and accordion headers.
* **Key UI Component Guidelines:**
  * **Navigation & Logo Placement:** `72px` header on `#FAF7F2` with the **Dovetail `A` Combination Mark** on the left, main navigation links in the center, and a flush-fitted CTA block on the right. Active navigation tabs display a `3px`-tall flared dovetail underline in `#824616`.
  * **Honest Pricing Cards & Comparison Tables:** Designed as "Flush-Fit Partnership vs. Expensive Local Hiring / Detached Agencies" cards on `#EFE9E0`. Contrasts local full-time developer employment costs—US `$196,500/yr` (`~$16,400/mo`) via [BLS May 2025](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/computer-and-information-technology/software-developers.htm) and [BLS June 2026](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/ecec.t04.htm), UK `£69,400/yr` (`~£5,800/mo`) via [ONS 2026](https://www.ons.gov.uk/employmentandlabourmarket/peopleinwork/earningsandworkinghours/bulletins/annualsurveyofhoursandearnings/2026) and [UK Gov 2026–27](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/rates-and-thresholds-for-employers-2026-to-2027), and Australia `A$147,800/yr` (`~A$12,300/mo`) via [Jobs and Skills Australia](https://www.jobsandskills.gov.au/data/occupation-and-industry-profiles/occupations-anzsco/2613-software-and-applications-programmers) and [ATO](https://www.ato.gov.au/tax-rates-and-codes/key-superannuation-rates-and-thresholds/super-guarantee)—alongside typical hourly agency billing ([Clutch Web Developers](https://clutch.co/web-developers/pricing), [Clutch Web Designers](https://clutch.co/web-designers/pricing), [Clutch SEO](https://clutch.co/seo-firms/pricing)) and per-seat/per-outcome pricing ([Microsoft 365 Copilot](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/copilot/enterprise), [Intercom](https://www.intercom.com/pricing), [Smith.ai](https://smith.ai/pricing/ai-receptionist)) against Alliance's published fixed quotes, 30-day cancellable monthly plans, full IP ownership (*"You own what we build"*), and the Philippines' `$40.3B` IT-BPM talent base ([IBPAP](https://admin.ibpap.org/storage/hub-resources/7Hv9U2uoLJVx2kxyRMObNm6W6L0MR57l9dZfrez7.pdf), [EF EPI 2025](https://www.ef.com/wwen/epi/regions/asia/philippines/), [Republic Act 10173](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/2/50253)).

## 5. Figma Best-Practices Verification Scorecard

* **App-Icon Scalability (`16×16px` & `60×60px`):** **PASS.** Consists of only **two solid interlocking polygons** separated by a single `2px` (`1.5px` at `16×16px`) flared negative-space kerf line. There are zero small floating fragments to blur at favicon size.
* **Monochrome (`1-Bit Black/White`) Test:** **PASS.** Because the flared dovetail joint is carved out by the continuous `2px` negative-space kerf channel between the upper and lower blocks, the interlocking dovetail silhouette reads at `100%` fidelity in 1-bit `#000000` on `#FFFFFF` and inverted `#FFFFFF` on `#000000`.
* **Readability & Contrast Audit:** **PASS.** Narra Hardwood Espresso (`#211814` on `#FAF7F2`) achieves `16.30:1` (WCAG AAA), Brass Marking-Gauge Ochre (`#824616` on `#FAF7F2`) achieves `6.93:1` (WCAG AA), and Workshop Slate-Green (`#235347` on `#FAF7F2`) achieves `8.19:1` (WCAG AAA).
