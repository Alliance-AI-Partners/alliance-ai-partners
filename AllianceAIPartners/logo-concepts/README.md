# Logo concepts (archived)

> [!NOTE]
> Archived exploration. None of these 15 concepts was chosen. The final logo is the 3D A+P ribbon mark in `docs/brand-kit/` and in the Figma file [Alliance AI Partners — Logo Suite](https://www.figma.com/design/rXNPaaMds46oWgM64QPRI6/Alliance-AI-Partners-%E2%80%94-Logo-Suite).

Each concept is a full written brief: a metaphor, a palette, fonts and the 12 logo types from Figma's guide to types of logos. Concept 1 (The Structural Keystone) was also drawn with two Gemini image models; those images are in `../Logos/concept-01-the-structural-keystone/`.

Don't use these concepts as guidance:

- Each one scores itself between 4.92 and 5.00 out of 5, so the scores don't tell them apart.
- Many use fonts on the banned list in [Design System Directions](../Design%20System%20Directions.md), such as Inter, Plus Jakarta Sans, Space Grotesk and Fraunces.
- Several build on ideas that [Big Idea Options](../Big%20Idea%20Options.md) had set aside, such as the keystone, the lighthouse and compass, and the relay.
- Concept 5 uses Philippine motifs, which the brand ruled out on 2 November 2026.
- Every concept has its own palette, none of which is the brand's.

The [Decision Log](../Decision%20Log.md) records what was chosen instead.
