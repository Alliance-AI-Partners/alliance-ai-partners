# Concept 11: The Equilibrium Scale & Fulcrum — "Simple on Purpose. Maximum Result."

**Brand Identity & Figma Logo System Specification · Alliance AI Partners (Brand DNA v2.1)**  
**Methodology Reference:** [Figma's 12 Types of Logos Framework](https://www.figma.com/resource-library/types-of-logos/)

## 1. Strategic Foundation & Logo Idea

* **Core Metaphor:** Grounded in Core Value #2 (**Simple on purpose**—*"We pick the simplest solution that does the job"*), Core Value #3 (*"If AI isn't the right answer, we say so, even when it means less work for us"*), and Elevator Pitch §6 (*"Two things let us charge less than most AI firms: AI does the repetitive work, and our team is based in the Philippines"*). Archimedes famously observed that with a firm **Fulcrum** and the right **Balance Beam**, a modest, precise input moves a massive load. Visually, a solid triangular architectural **Fulcrum** in Cast Iron Slate (`#1B2021`) supports a horizontal-to-cantilevered **Equilibrium Beam** in Fulcrum Copper (`#8C3D20`) and Counterweight Verdigris (`#1E5B53`) across its waist, while an upper structural truss completes the unmistakable silhouette of a monumental capital **`A`**. It communicates calm judgment, objective cost-benefit weighing, and structural efficiency: we never over-engineer when a simpler fix saves the partner money.
* **Master Mark Recommendation (Static vs. Dynamic):** **Hybrid Static-Fulcrum + Dynamic-Cantilever System.** The primary corporate mark uses a **Static Level-Equilibrium `A`** where the horizontal beam sits in calm, level structural balance across the triangular fulcrum. In interactive ROI calculators, pricing comparison tables, and dynamic headers (Type 12), the cantilever beam tilts or extends across the fulcrum to illustrate how a compact investment in an Alliance embedded team or automation balances out a massive local hiring cost or manual workload.
* **Primary Sketch / Geometry Concept:**
  * **Base Construction Grid:** Engineered on a `24×24px` square grid (`240×240px` vector master) with a `60°` equilateral triangular fulcrum base (`y = 14..22, x = 5..19`) and an upper `60°` architectural apex hood (`y = 2..10`).
  * **Central Cantilever Crossbar:** Between `y = 10` and `y = 14` (`2px` negative-space clearance above and below), a precision horizontal balance beam (`3px` thick) spans from `x = 2` to `x = 22`, resting directly on the sharp apex point of the lower triangular fulcrum.
  * **Asymmetrical Mechanical Advantage Accent:** The left tip of the beam features a compact `3×3px` Counterweight Verdigris (`#1E5B53`) block balancing a wide Fulcrum Copper (`#8C3D20`) right span—visually encoding "less input cost, greater business output" inside a clean capital **`A`**.

## 3. Design System & UI Integration Overview

* **Typography System:**
  * **Display / Headings:** **Plus Jakarta Sans** (`600 SemiBold`, `700 Bold`; Tracking: `-0.025em`; Line-Height: `1.14`). Perfectly balanced horizontal proportions and geometric counter-spaces mirror the equilibrium beam of the logo.
  * **Body & UI Controls:** **Inter** (Weights: `400 Regular`, `500 Medium`, `600 SemiBold`; Base Size: `16px`; Line-Height: `1.6`). Plain-English clarity that embodies Brand DNA Rule #1 (*"Start with the result"*) and Rule #2 (*"Listen first"*).
  * **Monospace / Pricing & Data:** **JetBrains Mono** (`500 Medium`, `600 SemiBold`). Used for side-by-side cost-benefit figures, hours-saved equations, and published starting prices.
* **Layout Grid & Spacing Tokens:**
  * **Desktop Grid:** `12-column` symmetrical balance grid (`1280px` max width, `24px` gutters) anchored by a central `x = 50%` vertical fulcrum axis—ideal for `6-column vs. 6-column` (*Instead of / Write*, *How Other Firms Charge / What We Do Instead*, *Local Hiring Cost / Alliance Embedded Developer*) comparisons.
  * **Spacing Scale:** `8px` spatial unit (`8px`, `16px`, `24px`, `32px`, `48px`, `64px`, `96px`).
  * **Border & Radius Tokens:** `4px` card radius, `2px` button radius, with a central `60°` triangular fulcrum pointer along horizontal section divider rules.
* **Key UI Component Guidelines:**
  * **Navigation & Logo Placement:** `72px` header on `#F9FAF8` featuring the **Equilibrium `A` Combination Mark** on the left, balanced center navigation links, and the Forged Fulcrum Copper (`#8C3D20`) primary CTA button on the right.
  * **Honest Pricing Cards & Comparison Tables:** Designed as an interactive **"True Cost Equilibrium Scale"** on `#EDEFEC`:
    * **Left Scale Pan (Market Benchmarks):** Displays verified third-party figures—US full-time developer employer cost of `~$196,500/yr` (`~$16,400/mo` via [BLS May 2025](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/computer-and-information-technology/software-developers.htm) and [BLS June 2026](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/ecec.t04.htm)), UK employer cost of `~£69,400/yr` (`~£5,800/mo` via [ONS 2026](https://www.ons.gov.uk/employmentandlabourmarket/peopleinwork/earningsandworkinghours/bulletins/annualsurveyofhoursandearnings/2026) and [UK Gov 2026–27](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/rates-and-thresholds-for-employers-2026-to-2027)), Australia employer cost of `~A$147,800/yr` (`~A$12,300/mo` via [Jobs and Skills Australia](https://www.jobsandskills.gov.au/data/occupation-and-industry-profiles/occupations-anzsco/2613-software-and-applications-programmers) and [ATO](https://www.ato.gov.au/tax-rates-and-codes/key-superannuation-rates-and-thresholds/super-guarantee)), agency hourly bands ([Clutch Web Developers](https://clutch.co/web-developers/pricing), [Clutch Web Designers](https://clutch.co/web-designers/pricing), [Clutch SEO](https://clutch.co/seo-firms/pricing)), and per-seat/per-outcome fees ([Microsoft 365 Copilot](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/copilot/enterprise), [Intercom](https://www.intercom.com/pricing), [Smith.ai](https://smith.ai/pricing/ai-receptionist)).

## 5. Figma Best-Practices Verification Scorecard

* **App-Icon Scalability (`16×16px` & `60×60px`):** **PASS.** Consists of 3 bold geometric shapes (upper `A` cap, horizontal cantilever crossbar, lower triangular fulcrum) separated by `2px` (`1.5px` at `16×16px`) negative-space gaps. Zero tiny hanging scale pans or thin chains that could blur at `16×16px`.
* **Monochrome (`1-Bit Black/White`) Test:** **PASS.** The `2px` negative-space clearance between the lower fulcrum apex, the horizontal beam, and the upper `A` hood preserves the complete fulcrum-and-beam silhouette in 1-bit `#000000` on `#FFFFFF`.
* **Readability & Contrast Audit:** **PASS.** All three primary colors pass **WCAG AAA (`> 7.1:1`)** on the light surface (`#F9FAF8`): Cast Iron Slate (`#1B2021`) at `15.73:1`, Forged Fulcrum Copper (`#8C3D20`) at `7.15:1`, and Counterweight Verdigris (`#1E5B53`) at `7.49:1`.
