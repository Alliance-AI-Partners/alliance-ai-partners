# Concept 1: The Structural Keystone — "Trust Holds Everything Up"

**Brand Identity & Figma Logo System Specification · Alliance AI Partners (Brand DNA v2.1)**  
**Methodology Reference:** [Figma's 12 Types of Logos Framework](https://www.figma.com/resource-library/types-of-logos/)

## 1. Strategic Foundation & Logo Idea

* **Core Metaphor:** In masonry and classical architecture, two separate columns or half-arches cannot bear load on their own until the central wedge—the **Keystone**—is dropped into the crown. Once the keystone locks into place, gravity and pressure bind the entire span into a self-supporting structure. For **Alliance AI Partners**, the left springing arch represents the partner company (in the US, UK, or Australia) and the right springing arch represents the embedded engineering, design, and growth team in the Philippines. The central trapezoidal keystone embodies our third core value—**Trust holds everything up**—as well as our combined **Sage + Ruler ("The Calm Architect")** persona. It signals that we do not peddle fragile "AI magic"; we drop into the crown of a partner's operations, lock their systems together, protect their data under the Philippines' [Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act 10173)](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/2/50253), and keep the entire structure standing for the long run.
* **Master Mark Recommendation (Static vs. Dynamic):** **Hybrid Static-Anchor + Dynamic-System.** The primary corporate mark (Type 7 Combination Mark and Type 4 Letterform) remains a rock-solid **Static Anchor** in Basalt Slate (`#16202A`) and Terracotta Keystone (`#9E3B24`) across legal contracts, compliance badges, invoices, and top-level navigation to project unwavering architectural permanence. In product headers, service landing pages, and interactive motion states (Type 10 Animated and Type 12 Dynamic), the central keystone wedge dynamically shifts its fill and internal sub-geometry to distinguish our three core service goals (*Scale AI*, *Solve problems with AI*, and *Grow*) while the outer load-bearing jambs never move.
* **Primary Sketch / Geometry Concept:**
  * **Base Construction Grid:** Engineered on a `24×24px` master icon grid (scaled 10× to `240×240px` for precision vector drafting) with a `2px` (`20pt` at `240px`) structural negative-space mortar channel separating all three stone blocks.
  * **Outer Arch Jambs (Left & Right):** Two symmetrical vertical-to-cantilevered voussoir blocks rising from `y = 22` to `y = 6`, with their inner faces cut at a crisp `72°` inward inclination (`18°` off vertical), subtly forming the outer legs of a monumental capital **`A`**.
  * **Central Keystone Wedge:** An inverted isosceles trapezoid suspended at the crown (`y = 2` to `y = 14`), whose angled side walls (`72°`) parallel the inner faces of the left and right jambs across a uniform `2px` mortar gap. The bottom face of the keystone forms the horizontal crossbar of the **`A`**, while the open semicircular vault beneath (`r = 5px`) creates an unmistakable architectural portal.
  * **Corner Radii & Stroke Rules:** Outer structural corners use a subtle `0.5px` (`5pt` at `240px`) optical chamfer to prevent harsh digital aliasing while preserving chiseled masonry authority. Minimum stroke/gap thickness is locked at `2px` at `24×24px` (`1.5px` at `16×16px` favicon scale).

## 3. Design System & UI Integration Overview

* **Typography System:**
  * **Display / Headings:** **Plus Jakarta Sans** (Weights: `600 SemiBold`, `700 Bold`; Tracking: `-0.025em` for H1/H2, `-0.015em` for H3; Line-Height: `1.15`). Its wide architectural stance, squared terminals, and clean geometric bowls mirror the tapered keystone silhouette without feeling cold or mechanical.
  * **Body & UI Controls:** **Inter** or **Public Sans** (Weights: `400 Regular`, `500 Medium`, `600 SemiBold`; Base Size: `16px` / `1rem`; Line-Height: `1.6` for long-form prose, `1.4` for UI tables; max line width `65ch`). Delivers effortless scannability for plain-English explanations ("Your orders now go straight into your system. Nobody types them twice.").
  * **Monospace / Pricing & Data:** **JetBrains Mono** (Weights: `500 Medium`, `600 SemiBold`; Tracking: `0em`; `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`). Used exclusively for published starting prices, fixed quotes, model usage pass-through invoices, and US/UK/AU vs. Philippines hourly/monthly cost comparison tables.
* **Layout Grid & Spacing Tokens:**
  * **Desktop Grid:** `12-column` structural grid, `1280px` max container width, `24px` column gutters, `64px` outer margins, with exposed `1px solid #D8D1C5` architectural rule lines dividing major page sections like stone course joints.
  * **Spacing Scale:** Strict `8px` spatial rhythm (`4px` micro-token for badge padding; `8px`, `16px`, `24px`, `32px`, `48px`, `64px`, `96px`, `128px` macro section spacing).
  * **Border & Radius Tokens:** Structural cards use a crisp `4px` border radius (`2px` for inline badges and buttons) and `1px` borders (`2px` active/selected borders) to reinforce chiseled masonry geometry rather than bubbly consumer SaaS pills.
* **Key UI Component Guidelines:**
  * **Navigation & Logo Placement:** Sticky `72px`-tall header on `#FAF8F5` (`95%` opacity with `8px` backdrop blur) anchored by a `1px` bottom mortar border (`#D8D1C5`). Desktop (`>=1024px`) displays the full Horizontal **Combination Mark** (`36px` symbol height + full `ALLIANCE AI PARTNERS` logotype). Tablet (`768px–1023px`) retains the Combination Mark at `32px`. Mobile (`<768px`) collapses smoothly to the standalone **Keystone Letterform (`A`)** (`32×32px`) on the left when scrolling, keeping the primary CTA button visible on the right.
  * **Honest Pricing Cards & Comparison Tables:** Designed as "Chiseled Specification Slabs" on `#F0ECE3` with a `4px` top border in Terracotta Keystone (`#9E3B24`). Every card leads with the plain-English outcome first, followed by the published starting price in `28px` **JetBrains Mono**, a bulleted checklist of included deliverables (project management, QA testing, new-hire-ready documentation, zero per-seat fees), and an expandable source-verified comparison drawer contrasting local full-time employer costs—US `$196,500/yr` (`~$16,400/mo`) via [BLS May 2025](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/computer-and-information-technology/software-developers.htm) and [BLS June 2026 benefits](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/ecec.t04.htm), UK `£69,400/yr` (`~£5,800/mo`) via [ONS 2026](https://www.ons.gov.uk/employmentandlabourmarket/peopleinwork/earningsandworkinghours/bulletins/annualsurveyofhoursandearnings/2026) and [UK Gov 2026–27 NI/pension](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/rates-and-thresholds-for-employers-2026-to-2027), and Australia `A$147,800/yr` (`~A$12,300/mo`) via [Jobs and Skills Australia](https://www.jobsandskills.gov.au/data/occupation-and-industry-profiles/occupations-anzsco/2613-software-and-applications-programmers) and [ATO 12% Super](https://www.ato.gov.au/tax-rates-and-codes/key-superannuation-rates-and-thresholds/super-guarantee)—alongside typical agency hourly rates ([Clutch Web Developers](https://clutch.co/web-developers/pricing), [Clutch Web Designers](https://clutch.co/web-designers/pricing), [Clutch SEO](https://clutch.co/seo-firms/pricing)) and commercial per-seat/per-outcome benchmarks ([Microsoft 365 Copilot](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/copilot/enterprise), [Intercom Fin](https://www.intercom.com/pricing), [Smith.ai](https://smith.ai/pricing/ai-receptionist)).
  * **Service Pillar Cards (Scale, Solve, Grow):** Implements Note 1 of the Brand DNA ("lead with the three goals and let people open the full list from there"). Three equal-width architectural arches sit side by side on the homepage: Pillar 1 (*Scale AI*) uses a Basalt Slate keystone (`#16202A`), Pillar 2 (*Solve problems with AI*) uses a Terracotta Keystone (`#9E3B24`), and Pillar 3 (*Grow*) uses a Patina Bronze-Green keystone (`#1F5A4C`). Clicking any pillar expands an organized accordion of sub-services without overwhelming first-time visitors.
  * **Buttons & Interactive States:** Primary CTA (*"See where AI can help"*) uses `#9E3B24` background with `#FAF8F5` text (`6.38:1` AA), `14px 24px` padding, `2px` border radius, and a subtle `2px` bottom structural shadow in `#6B2515`. **Hover:** Darkens to `#7F2E1B` (`8.12:1` AAA) with a `1px` upward lift. **Keyboard Focus:** High-visibility `2px` solid `#16202A` outer ring with a `2px` `#FAF8F5` offset (`15.55:1` contrast). **Active:** Depresses `1px` downward, collapsing the bottom shadow.
