# Surface brief: brand kit showcase

Scope: `brand-kit/index.html`, the showcase of the Alliance AI Partners brand kit and design system, plus the templates, emails and merch pages it links. Visitor mode: Experience (the brand's own assets lead from the first viewport).

Audience and job: developers building the Next.js site (tokens, components, assets), designers and marketers (templates, imagery), print and merch vendors (print-ready files and specs), partners and press (logo, colours, one-page overview). Each comes for one asset or rule and leaves with it.

Action: download or copy the asset they came for; the primary action is "Download the logo kit".

Proof and content: the finalized logo suite, palette, brand graphics and swallow mascot (docs/brand-kit); real templates, emails and merch built in this kit. No invented partners, prices or results.

Constraints: PRODUCT.md brand commitments, the logo kit is the only visual source, WCAG AA text, no usual-AI-look devices.

Chosen direction: "The Ribbon", user-pinned from the logo kit. Memorable moment: the Daybreak ribbon leaving the mark and threading the whole page.

Unresolved: image generation waits for GEMINI_API_KEY; with it, this showcase is comp-led (recorded default `comp`), without it code-led.

## Direction contract

THESIS: One continuous Daybreak ribbon (Partner Navy to Alliance Blue to Ribbon Cyan) threads the page and ties every section together, the way it binds the A and the P. It refuses the AI-agency default of a dark grid, glow and three feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: Daylight ground with whole Partner Navy chapters; Partner Navy type; Alliance Blue only for the one committing action in view; Ribbon Cyan as the ribbon's light. Depth comes from the mark's eight shading tokens lit from the top left, never glow or glass. Mona Sans: Expanded semibold display with negative tracking over normal-width text; Mona Sans Mono for sourced notes, times and codes. Imagery is nature (swallows, leaves, flowers) formed from ribbon and facets, never regional motifs.

STORY: The visitor meets the brand as a working system: the mark, then colour, type, imagery, applications (emails, stationery, merch) and downloads. They come away believing Alliance is a skilled, accountable partner rather than cheap help, holding the asset they came for.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left half: "Brand kit" in Expanded display, a one-line purpose, "Download the logo kit" in Alliance Blue and "Read the design system" as a quiet link. Right half: the 3D mark large on Daylight, its ribbon sweeping out across the viewport behind the title and down into the palette section below the fold.
