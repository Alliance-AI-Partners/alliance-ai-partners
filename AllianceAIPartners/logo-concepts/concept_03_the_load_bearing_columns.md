# Concept 3: The Load-Bearing Columns — "Scale AI. Solve Problems. Grow."

**Brand Identity & Figma Logo System Specification · Alliance AI Partners (Brand DNA v2.1)**  
**Methodology Reference:** [Figma's 12 Types of Logos Framework](https://www.figma.com/resource-library/types-of-logos/)

## 1. Strategic Foundation & Logo Idea

* **Core Metaphor:** Directly visualizes the structural backbone of **Alliance AI Partners** defined in *Who We Are* and *Note 1 before launch* (*"On the website and in sales calls, lead with the three goals—scale AI, solve problems, and grow—and let people open the full list from there"*). Three vertical modernist load-bearing columns—representing **1. Scale AI**, **2. Solve problems with AI**, and **3. Grow**—stand on a unified foundation plinth and support a shared horizontal entablature/lintel that angles upward at the crown to form an architectural capital **`A`**. It communicates our **Sage + Ruler ("The Calm Architect")** persona: when a partner brings us into their business, we don't hand over a brittle script and disappear; we erect three permanent load-bearing pillars under their operations and stay to keep the whole roof standing.
* **Master Mark Recommendation (Static vs. Dynamic):** **Hybrid Static-Entablature + Dynamic-Column System.** The three columns and their shared foundation/lintel remain a permanent **Static Structural Frame** in corporate headers and legal marks. Across service pages, proposals, and interactive dashboards, the three columns act as a **Dynamic Architectural Index** (Type 12): whichever service goal the user is exploring (*Column 1: Scale AI*, *Column 2: Solve problems with AI*, or *Column 3: Grow*) illuminates in Doric Ochre-Bronze (`#7A4918`) or Ionic Teal (`#185866`) and elevates slightly while the other two columns remain grounded in Classical Obsidian (`#1A1917`).
* **Primary Sketch / Geometry Concept:**
  * **Base Construction Grid:** Built on a `24×24px` modular grid (`240×240px` vector master) governed by classical `1 : 1.618` golden-section proportions and a uniform `2px` (`20pt`) column-to-intercolumniation ratio (`3px` column width, `2px` negative-space colonnade gap).
  * **Three Vertical Shafts:** Three parallel vertical pillars occupy `x = 5..8`, `x = 10.5..13.5`, and `x = 16..19`, rising from a shared `2px`-tall stylobate base (`y = 20..22`).
  * **Pitched Pediment / Lintel (`A` Crown):** Above the shafts (`y = 2..9`), a unified triangular-trapezoidal pediment spans all three columns, its inner negative space carved into a crisp horizontal lintel slot so the overall silhouette reads simultaneously as a classical three-column colonnade and a monumental modernist letter **`A`**.
  * **Corner & Terminal Rules:** Crisp `90°` architectural corners (`0.25px` optical softening at print scale) with zero decorative flutes or ornate Corinthian scrolls that would blur at small sizes.

## 3. Design System & UI Integration Overview

* **Typography System:**
  * **Display / Headings:** **Instrument Serif** or **Newsreader** (`500 Medium` / `600 SemiBold`, tracking `-0.02em`, line-height `1.12`) paired with **Plus Jakarta Sans** (`700 Bold`) for structural subheaders. Pairing an authoritative editorial/architectural serif on major statements (*"Put AI to work."*, *"The AI team you don't have to hire."*) with a crisp geometric sans-serif immediately separates Alliance AI Partners from every lookalike SaaS agency and reinforces the **Sage + Ruler** archetype.
  * **Body & UI Controls:** **Inter** (Weights: `400 Regular`, `500 Medium`, `600 SemiBold`; Base Size: `16px`; Line-Height: `1.6`). Keeps complex technical explanations (e.g., *AI connectors (MCP)*, *Digital twins*, *Fixing AI-built apps*) plain, calm, and accessible.
  * **Monospace / Pricing & Data:** **JetBrains Mono** (`500 Medium`). Used for column indices (`I · SCALE AI`, `II · SOLVE PROBLEMS`, `III · GROW`), published starting prices, and verified US/UK/AU salary benchmarks.
* **Layout Grid & Spacing Tokens:**
  * **Desktop Grid:** `12-column` symmetrical colonnade grid (`1280px` max width, `24px` gutters) that naturally divides into **three `4-column` structural bays**—mirroring the three columns of the logo across every major landing page section.
  * **Spacing Scale:** `8px` modular grid (`8px`, `16px`, `24px`, `32px`, `48px`, `64px`, `96px`) with exposed `1px solid #DCD6CA` vertical colonnade dividers between the three `4-column` bays on desktop viewports.
  * **Border & Radius Tokens:** `2px` sharp architectural radii on cards and buttons (`0px` on full-bleed structural tables) with a `3px` solid top lintel border on primary containers.

## 5. Figma Best-Practices Verification Scorecard

* **App-Icon Scalability (`16×16px` & `60×60px`):** **PASS.** At `60×60px`, all three columns, the base plinth, and the pitched `A` pediment render with crisp `3px` intercolumniations. At `16×16px`, the three vertical shafts snap exactly to pixel columns (`x = 3..5`, `x = 7..9`, `x = 11..13`) with `2px` clear gaps between them, ensuring zero anti-aliasing blur.
* **Monochrome (`1-Bit Black/White`) Test:** **PASS.** Because the three columns are separated by `2px` full-height negative-space channels and framed by a distinct horizontal base plinth and pitched roof, the mark retains 100% structural clarity in pure `#000000` on `#FFFFFF` and inverted `#FFFFFF` on `#000000`.
* **Readability & Contrast Audit:** **PASS.** Every core color passes WCAG AAA on the primary light background (`#FBF9F6`): Classical Obsidian (`#1A1917`) at `16.72:1`, Doric Ochre-Bronze (`#7A4918`) at `7.15:1`, and Ionic Structural Teal (`#185866`) at `7.61:1`.
