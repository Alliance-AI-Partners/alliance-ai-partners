# Concept 12: The Twin-Chevron Relay — "Partners in AI. Partners in Growth."

**Brand Identity & Figma Logo System Specification · Alliance AI Partners (Brand DNA v2.1)**  
**Methodology Reference:** [Figma's 12 Types of Logos Framework](https://www.figma.com/resource-library/types-of-logos/)

## 1. Strategic Foundation & Logo Idea

* **Core Metaphor:** Built directly from Taglines §7 (*"Partners in AI. Partners in growth."*—explicitly designated in the Brand DNA table for *"Logo lockup, email signature"*, plus *"We build it, run it, and help you grow"* and *"Brief us before you log off. See progress when you log on."*). Visually, **two synchronized ascending chevrons (`^` + `^`)**—representing **1. Partners in AI** (the technical/automation engine) and **2. Partners in Growth** (the revenue/marketing engine), as well as the partner's in-house team and our Philippines team—nest and overlap along a shared diagonal relay zone (`60°`) to form a bold, forward-driving capital **`A`**. Where the lower chevron's right leg meets the upper chevron's left leg, a Baton Forest Green (`#1D633B`) relay bar forms the horizontal crossbar of the **`A`**, symbolizing the seamless handoff where agencies usually stop and Alliance AI Partners keeps running.
* **Master Mark Recommendation (Static vs. Dynamic):** **Hybrid Static-Nested-Chevron + Dynamic-Relay System.** The primary corporate mark uses a **Static Nested Twin-Chevron `A`** paired with the signature lockup tagline *"Partners in AI. Partners in growth."* Across interactive web states, sprint trackers, and service headers (Type 12), the twin chevrons step upward or forward in a phased relay cadence to represent *Scale AI*, *Solve problems with AI*, and *Grow*.
* **Primary Sketch / Geometry Concept:**
  * **Base Construction Grid:** Engineered on a `24×24px` geometric grid (`240×240px` vector master) built on `60°` equilateral angles with `3.5px`-wide chevron ribbons and a `2px` (`20pt`) negative-space relay channel.
  * **Upper Chevron (`A` Crown — `#131921`):** Rises from `x = 4, y = 17` to the apex at `x = 12, y = 2` and descends to `x = 20, y = 17`.
  * **Lower Nested Relay Chevron (`A` Base & Crossbar — `#124E96` & `#1D633B`):** Rises nested `2px` inside and below the upper chevron (`x = 6, y = 22` to `x = 12, y = 10` to `x = 18, y = 22`), with its flattened apex (`y = 11..14`) extending horizontally in `#1D633B` to lock into the outer chevron legs as the crossbar of the **`A`**.

## 3. Design System & UI Integration Overview

* **Typography System:**
  * **Display / Headings:** **Plus Jakarta Sans** or **Sora** (Weights: `600 SemiBold`, `700 Bold`; Tracking: `-0.025em`; Line-Height: `1.14`). Crisp diagonal terminals in `A`, `V`, `W`, `M`, and `K` echo the `60°` nested chevrons of the logo.
  * **Body & UI Controls:** **Inter** (Weights: `400 Regular`, `500 Medium`, `600 SemiBold`; Base Size: `16px`; Line-Height: `1.6`). Plain-English clarity that keeps every sentence focused on partner outcomes.
  * **Monospace / Pricing & Data:** **JetBrains Mono** (`500 Medium`). Used for weekly sprint counters, published starting prices, and verified US/UK/AU employer cost tables.
* **Layout Grid & Spacing Tokens:**
  * **Desktop Grid:** `12-column` relay grid (`1280px` max width, `24px` gutters) featuring stepped horizontal "Exchange Zone" connectors between consecutive page sections so the user's eye flows from *Understand -> Plan -> Build -> Run and grow*.
  * **Spacing Scale:** `8px` spatial unit (`8px`, `16px`, `24px`, `32px`, `48px`, `64px`, `96px`).
  * **Border & Radius Tokens:** `4px` card radius, `2px` button radius, with signature double-chevron (`>>`) list markers and breadcrumb dividers.
* **Key UI Component Guidelines:**
  * **Navigation & Logo Placement:** `72px` header on `#F8FAFC` featuring the **Twin-Chevron `A` Combination Mark** with its integrated lockup tagline (*"Partners in AI. Partners in growth."*) on the left, clean navigation links in the center, and the primary CTA button on the right.
  * **Honest Pricing Cards & Comparison Tables:** Designed as a "Where Other Agencies Stop vs. Where Alliance Stays" comparison ledger on `#EBF0F5`. Contrasts traditional agency models—hourly rates (`US$100–$149/hr` in US/AU via [Clutch Web Developers](https://clutch.co/web-developers/pricing) & [Clutch Web Designers](https://clutch.co/web-designers/pricing)), per-seat tools (`US$30/user/mo` via [Microsoft 365 Copilot](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/copilot/enterprise)), per-outcome bots (`US$0.99/outcome` via [Intercom](https://www.intercom.com/pricing) and `US$150/mo + $2.50/call` via [Smith.ai](https://smith.ai/pricing/ai-receptionist)), and retainers (`US$3,199/mo` avg via [Clutch SEO](https://clutch.co/seo-firms/pricing))—plus local full-time employer costs in the US (`~$196,500/yr` via [BLS](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/computer-and-information-technology/software-developers.htm) & [BLS ECEC](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/ecec.t04.htm)), UK (`~£69,400/yr` via [ONS](https://www.ons.gov.uk/employmentandlabourmarket/peopleinwork/earningsandworkinghours/bulletins/annualsurveyofhoursandearnings/2026) & [UK Gov](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/rates-and-thresholds-for-employers-2026-to-2027)), and Australia (`~A$147,800/yr` via [Jobs and Skills Australia](https://www.jobsandskills.gov.au/data/occupation-and-industry-profiles/occupations-anzsco/2613-software-and-applications-programmers) & [ATO](https://www.ato.gov.au/tax-rates-and-codes/key-superannuation-rates-and-thresholds/super-guarantee)) against Alliance's published fixed quotes, backed by the Philippines' `$40.3B` IT-BPM industry ([IBPAP](https://admin.ibpap.org/storage/hub-resources/7Hv9U2uoLJVx2kxyRMObNm6W6L0MR57l9dZfrez7.pdf)), #2 Asian English ranking ([EF EPI 2025](https://www.ef.com/wwen/epi/regions/asia/philippines/)), and [Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173)](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/2/50253).
  * **Service Pillar Cards (Scale, Solve, Grow):** Styled as a **Three-Stage Relay Track**: *Stage 1: Scale AI* (`#124E96`), *Stage 2: Solve problems with AI* (`#131921`), and *Stage 3: Grow* (`#1D633B`).
  * **Buttons & Interactive States:** Primary CTA (*"See where AI can help"*) uses Relay Kinetic Blue (`#124E96`) with `#F8FAFC` text (`7.85:1` AAA) and a double-chevron (`>>`) icon that shifts `4px` right on hover. **Focus:** `2px` solid `#131921` outer ring with `2px` `#F8FAFC` offset (`16.88:1` AAA).

## 5. Figma Best-Practices Verification Scorecard

* **App-Icon Scalability (`16×16px` & `60×60px`):** **PASS.** Nested `60°` chevrons are among the most legible geometric shapes in digital UI design. At `16×16px`, the outer `#131921` chevron and inner `#124E96`/`#1D633B` chevron maintain a crisp `1.5px` separation channel with zero visual clutter.
* **Monochrome (`1-Bit Black/White`) Test:** **PASS.** The `2px` negative-space channel between the upper chevron, lower chevron, and horizontal baton bar ensures all elements read cleanly in 1-bit `#000000` on `#FFFFFF`.
* **Readability & Contrast Audit:** **PASS.** Track Asphalt Ink (`#131921` on `#F8FAFC`) achieves `16.88:1` (WCAG AAA), Relay Kinetic Blue (`#124E96` on `#F8FAFC`) achieves `7.85:1` (WCAG AAA), and Baton Forest Green (`#1D633B` on `#F8FAFC`) achieves `6.92:1` (WCAG AA).
