## 1. Strategic Foundation & Logo Idea

* **Core Metaphor:** Directly inspired by Brand Archetype §5 (*"Together they make us something like an architect, someone who understands the whole structure and keeps it standing"*), Core Value #2 (*"We write down how everything works, clearly enough for a new hire to follow"*), and Elevator Pitch §6 (*"Our honest pricing promise: Published starting prices for every core service. A fixed quote before work starts that lists everything we'll charge"*). On every architectural and civil engineering blueprint, the lower-right corner is anchored by the **Title Block**—a ruled structural grid where the architect signs their name, states the exact scale, lists the materials, and certifies the tolerances. We turn the **Architectural Title Block & Drafting Compass `A`** into the master visual metaphor: a precision square ledger frame (`24×24px`) subdivided by golden-ratio drafting rules, enclosing a bold structural **`A`** with a Ledger Vermilion (`#A62B2B`) verification stamp block at the crossbar. It tells every US, UK, and Australian founder: *Nothing is hidden here. Our specs, our code documentation, and our prices are laid out on the table.*
* **Master Mark Recommendation (Static vs. Dynamic):** **Hybrid Static-Stamp + Dynamic-Ledger System.** The primary corporate mark uses a **Static Architectural Ledger `A`** where the outer title-block border and inner drafting `A` form a unified seal of engineering accountability. Across service pages, proposals, invoices, and live documentation, the mark expands into a **Dynamic Title Block System** (Type 12) where the sub-cells of the ledger dynamically populate with the active service code (`SCL-01` for *Scale AI*, `SLV-02` for *Solve problems with AI*, `GRW-03` for *Grow*), the verified starting price, or the partner's time-zone overlap.
* **Primary Sketch / Geometry Concept:**
  * **Base Construction Grid:** Engineered on a `24×24px` Swiss modular grid (`240×240px` vector master) enclosed by a `2px` (`20pt`) outer square frame (`x = 2..22, y = 2..22`) with `90°` mitered architectural corners.
  * **Internal Title-Block Subdivisions:** A horizontal datum rule cuts across at `y = 16`, dividing the square into an upper elevation chamber (`14px` tall) and a lower specification ledger bar (`6px` tall, subdivided at `x = 15` into a `2:1` ratio).
  * **Drafting Compass `A` Geometry:** Inside the upper chamber, two bold `3px` diagonal drafting legs rise from the datum line (`x = 5, y = 16` and `x = 19, y = 16`) to the top border (`x = 12, y = 2`), while the lower-right cell (`x = 15..22, y = 16..22`) is filled with a solid Ledger Vermilion (`#A62B2B`) verification block—making the whole mark read simultaneously as a capital **`A`** and a stamped engineering blueprint title block.

## 3. Design System & UI Integration Overview

* **Typography System:**
  * **Display / Headings:** **Space Grotesk** or **Plus Jakarta Sans** (`600 SemiBold`, `700 Bold`; Tracking: `-0.025em`; Line-Height: `1.14`). Technical, squared-off architectural letterforms that sit naturally inside exposed Swiss grid cells.
  * **Body & UI Controls:** **IBM Plex Sans** (Weights: `400 Regular`, `500 Medium`, `600 SemiBold`; Base Size: `16px`; Line-Height: `1.58`). Engineered specifically for technical clarity and plain-English documentation ("We write down how everything works, clearly enough for a new hire to follow").
  * **Monospace / Pricing & Data:** **IBM Plex Mono** (Weights: `500 Medium`, `600 SemiBold`; `tabular-nums`). Used for blueprint title-block metadata (`REV 2.1 · OCT 2026`), published starting prices, model fee pass-throughs, and regional salary comparison tables.
* **Layout Grid & Spacing Tokens:**
  * **Desktop Grid:** Exposed `12-column` Swiss architectural blueprint grid (`1280px` max width, `0px` collapsed border-collapse cells or `24px` padded bays separated by visible `1px solid #B8C7DC` hairline rules and `+` crosshair intersection ticks).
  * **Spacing Scale:** Strict `8px` drafting module (`8px`, `16px`, `24px`, `32px`, `48px`, `64px`, `96px`).
  * **Border & Radius Tokens:** `0px` to `2px` sharp drafting corners; every major section includes a mini **Title Block Header Strip** (`28px` tall) displaying the section number, plain-English outcome promise, and verification source link.
* **Key UI Component Guidelines:**
  * **Navigation & Logo Placement:** Top header (`72px`) styled as an architectural drawing header strip with visible `1px` vertical cell dividers between the **Combination Mark** cell (left), the three Service Pillar cells (*01 Scale AI*, *02 Solve Problems*, *03 Grow*), the *Published Prices* cell, and the full-bleed Ledger Vermilion (`#A62B2B`) CTA cell on the far right.
  * **Honest Pricing Cards & Comparison Tables:** The centerpiece of Concept 6. Rendered as an authentic **Engineering Bill of Materials & Cost Specification Sheet**:

## 5. Figma Best-Practices Verification Scorecard

* **App-Icon Scalability (`16×16px` & `60×60px`):** **PASS.** Because the outer perimeter is already a `1:1` square (`24×24px` grid), it maps `1:1` into a `60×60px` app icon and a `16×16px` browser favicon with zero wasted corner space. At `16×16px`, the internal vertical cell divider simplifies to a single `2px` border around the `A` and a `4×4px` solid `#A62B2B` lower-right stamp block.
* **Monochrome (`1-Bit Black/White`) Test:** **PASS.** All cells in the title block are separated by `2px` negative-space or stroke rules, so the outer frame, upper `A`, and lower-right verification block remain 100% distinct in 1-bit `#000000` on `#FFFFFF`.
* **Readability & Contrast Audit:** **PASS.** Prussian Drafting Ink (`#0C1D36` on `#F7F9FC`) achieves `16.00:1` (WCAG AAA), Cyanotype Cobalt (`#154D99` on `#F7F9FC`) achieves `7.80:1` (WCAG AAA), and Ledger Stamp Vermilion (`#A62B2B` on `#F7F9FC`) achieves `6.63:1` (WCAG AA).
