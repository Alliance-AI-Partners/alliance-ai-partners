## 01 Abstract Logo

Model: gemini-3-pro-image, 1:1, 2K

Minimalist flat vector Abstract Logo for a B2B enterprise technology partner named ALLIANCE AI PARTNERS. Subject & Metaphor: Three bold geometric polygons forming an architectural keystone arch through Gestalt closure—two outer canted trapezoid pillars in Basalt Slate (#16202A) and one central suspended trapezoidal keystone wedge in Terracotta (#9E3B24) separated by crisp negative-space channels, subtly forming a monumental letter A. Color Palette: High-contrast pairing of Basalt Slate #16202A and Terracotta #9E3B24 on a solid unblemished Travertine Warm White #FAF8F5 background. Design Principles: Designed by a Swiss modernist brand studio (Pentagram / Chermayeff & Geismar aesthetic), bold figure-ground contrast, scalable down to a 16x16px app icon, works in pure black-and-white monochrome. Negative Constraints: Flat 2D vector graphic, no text, no gradients, no AI sparkles, no glowing brains, no circuit board lines, no robot faces, no purple-or-pink neon, no mockups, no realistic photo backgrounds, no watermarks.
